Section 01

Machine Envelope & Structural Architecture

Designed for production fabrication shops cutting raw 20-foot (6-meter) structural mill sticks without pre-cutting on a cold saw.

STRUCTURAL FOUNDATION

5" × 3" × 1/4" Structural Steel Spine

The machine bed is built upon a single, continuous 6-meter 5.0" × 3.0" × 1/4" (127 × 76 × 6.35 mm) rectangular HSS structural tube. This main structural backbone provides immense torsional rigidity, preventing bed sag under heavy thick-wall pipe and resisting high dynamic acceleration forces from rapid gantry reversals.

Heavy-duty adjustable leveling pedestals spaced along the spine allow precision laser-level alignment across the entire 20-foot bed length, isolating the machine from uneven shop floors.

KINEMATICS OVERVIEW

True 4-Axis Synchronized Motion

Unlike 2-axis rotary attachments that disconnect a flat-table Y-axis, this machine features true dedicated 4-axis simultaneous kinematics:

  • X-Axis (Longitudinal): 6,096 mm (20 ft) travel along the bed length for cutting mill sticks.
  • Y-Axis (Transverse): 1,000 mm (3.3 ft) cross-slide carriage for cutting across wide pipe profiles, slots, and off-center holes.
  • Z-Axis (Vertical Lifter): Fast initial height sensing (IHS) retracts and active THC arc voltage tracking.
  • A-Axis (Rotary): Continuous 360° stock rotation driven by dual synchronized chucks.
Section 02

Motion Controller & SimCNC 4D Feedrate Scaling

Industrial motion hardware paired with CSLab SimCNC and RotaryCAM's proprietary 4D trajectory feedrate scaling.

HARDWARE CONTROLLER

CSLab CSMIO/IP-M 4-Axis Controller

The control cabinet is centered around the CSLab CSMIO/IP-M Ethernet 4-axis motion controller. It generates rock-solid 125 kHz step/direction signals over galvanic Ethernet, eliminating PC jitter and USB dropouts.

Hardware-level 24V industrial opto-isolated I/O guarantees complete immunity against high-voltage electrical noise, pilot arc RF interference, and plasma high-frequency start spikes.

ROTARYCAM 4D FEEDRATE

The Rotary Feedrate Problem & CAM Solution

Standard CNC controllers (including SimCNC) calculate feedrate (F) in linear units-per-minute (inches/min or mm/min) or pure rotary degrees-per-minute. They cannot natively calculate constant surface speed when blending linear X/Y moves with rotary A-axis rotation.

If you cut an X-slot at F100, the torch travels at 100 IPM. But when cutting around a 2-inch pipe circumference, pure rotary motion at the same F drops or spikes surface speed, causing severe dross or arc extinguish. On square tube, the surface radius constantly changes between flat faces and corners!

How RotaryCAM Solves This

RotaryCAM pre-computes 4D Euclidean trajectory feedrate scaling directly into the generated G-code. By calculating instantaneous surface radii for every arc segment, RotaryCAM scales feedrates dynamically so the plasma jet cuts at an exact, uniform surface speed (IPM) across round pipe, square tube faces, and corner radii.

Section 04

Dual Rotary Headstocks & Crossed Roller Slew Rings

Eliminating 20-foot torsional whip and slag contamination with industrial through-bore rotary mechanics.

FRONT HEADSTOCK

XSU080318 Crossed Roller Slew Bearing

At the cutting front where hot molten slag, dross, and sparks shower downward, conventional spindly bearings fail rapidly. This machine utilizes an XSU080318 high-rigidity crossed roller slewing ring bearing.

Its alternating cylindrical rollers withstand massive simultaneous radial, axial, and moment loads, allowing thick-wall pipe to rotate smoothly with zero radial play and zero deflection under torch downward pressure.

REAR CHUCK & REDUCER

ZCT200 10:1 Hollow Actuator + K72 Chuck

The drive actuator is a ZCT200 10:1 hollow rotary platform featuring zero-backlash planetary gearing and a massive central through-bore for long pipe pass-through.

Fitted with a heavy-duty K72 4-jaw independent chuck with reversible hardened jaws, allowing secure centering of round pipe, square tubing, rectangular HSS, and even asymmetric angle iron.

Section 05

Precision Linear Guides & Helical Rack Drives

Continuous helical tooth engagement eliminates the tooth impact chatter and scalloped cut edges of cheap spur racks.

X & Y AXIS GUIDES

HGH25 Heavy-Load Profile Linear Rails

Both the 6-meter bed carriage and the 1-meter cross slide ride on precision-ground HGH25 linear profile rails with heavy-load flanged recirculating ball carriage blocks.

Linear rails are bolted directly to the precision-machined top reference surface of the HSS structural spine, ensuring perfect parallelism and zero gantry bind across all 20 feet of travel.

TRANSMISSION

Module 1.5 Hardened Helical Rack & Pinion

Standard straight spur racks vibrate as teeth enter and exit mesh, creating distinct ripple marks on plasma cuts. This machine uses precision-ground module 1.5 helical rack and pinion.

Helical teeth maintain continuous, overlapping tooth contact at all times, providing whisper-quiet motion, high positional accuracy, and mirror-clean plasma edge finishes.

Section 06

Torch Lifter, Dual IHS & Safety Systems

Zero-deflection ohmic sensing, mechanical floating head backup, and high-speed corner anti-dive locking.

PRIMARY IHS

FastCutCNC Ohmic Sensing

A dedicated FastCutCNC ohmic sensing isolator box couples directly to the Hypertherm machine torch shield cap. Detects plate contact via electrical continuity with zero downward mechanical force, preventing thin-wall tube deflection during pierce referencing.

BACKUP IHS

Floating Head Microswitch

A precision mechanical floating head slider sits behind the torch mount. If heavy mill scale, rust, or cutting oil prevents ohmic contact, the floating head switch triggers instantly as a mechanical fail-safe before the Z servo stalls.

SAFETY & COLLISION

Magnetic Breakaway & Anti-Dive

Magnetic Breakaway: High-force neodymium magnets hold the torch carrier. If a cut pipe slug tips up, the torch snaps off cleanly and trips an E-stop circuit.

THC Anti-Dive: RotaryCAM outputs M62/M63 high-speed digital outputs to freeze THC before decelerating around corners, stopping torch dives.
