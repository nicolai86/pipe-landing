01 // Why Software THCs Dive

Your Z motor isn’t too slow. Your CNC controller’s motion buffer is in the way.

Most PC and Ethernet CNCs route arc voltage through a 15–40 ms trajectory buffer. By the time software reacts, the torch has already dived into a corner or crashed into bowed plate.

True Hardware Summing-Junction Interceptor

Intercepts Z Step/Dir inline while passing X/Y/A/B/C straight through in PCB copper (0 ns latency). Zero CNC plugins. During a cut, physical Z tracks plate warps independently of your CNC’s Z DRO, then unwinds its step offset back to host Z at M5.

02 // Six-Layer Crash Protection

Built for warped steel, tipped-up slugs, and worn electrodes.

Every protection layer runs inside the 10 kHz hardware loop in silicon. Tested on live flat-sheet and 20-foot rotary tables.

2.0 ms Silicon Trigger

Active In-Cut Ohmic Collision Escape

Voltage THCs go blind during corner freezes and kerf crossings. A single wire tee off your 24V ohmic shield (J3-2) overrides all freezes and commands an instant retract in silicon within 2.0 ms when a tipped slug touches the shield.

0.001″ travel @ 30 IPM • +0.500″ (12.7 mm) lift cap
Zero Macros Needed

Macro-Free Corner, Hole & Kerf Anti-Dive

Reads live step pulses and locks downward Z automatically when speed drops below 80% of cruise or deceleration hits 0.08–0.10 G. No post-processor macros or M-codes needed, even on legacy G-code.

Up to 295 IPM UP (ClearPath 2311S) / 75 IPM DOWN • Auto-adapts to FRO

Unlike software THCs clamped to ±0.5″, this board unlocks 100% of your lifter’s physical stroke with zero artificial limits. A 1 kHz Bessel filter and upward slope integrator track severe warps smoothly, then unwind the injected Z step offset back to host Z at M5.

05 // 20 KB Black-Box Flight Recorder & smart-thc CLI

Replace shop-floor guesswork with a 10,000 Hz digital oscilloscope.

When a cut ends or a fault trips, the board freezes 1,024 synchronized frames (20 KB) in SRAM with 32-bit machine coordinates so you can inspect the exact cut trajectory.

Zero-Dependency Go Binary

Cross-Platform smart-thc Tooling

Single static binary for macOS, Linux, and Windows. Connects over USB-RS485 or an Ethernet TCP bridge.

  • ▸smart-thc dump -ascii-plot: ASCII arc waveform and 46-column timestamped CSV with protection timing.
  • ▸smart-thc jog up|down <dist>: Direct bench jogging in inches or mm with safety interlocks.
Unbrickable Page-0 Bootstub
