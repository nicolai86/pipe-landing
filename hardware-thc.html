01 // Why Software THCs Dive

Your Z motor isn’t too slow. Your CNC controller’s motion buffer is in the way.

Most PC and Ethernet motion controllers route arc voltage through a 15–40 ms trajectory lookahead buffer. Because the height loop is always reacting late, you have to turn loop gains down 4–5× just to stop the torch from bouncing—guaranteeing sluggish climbs up warped sheet and violent dives into corners and kerfs.

True Hardware Summing-Junction Interceptor

Sits directly inline on the Z Step/Dir cable while passing X/Y/A/B/C straight through in PCB copper (0 ns added latency). Zero CNC plugins, zero lost DRO steps.

02 // Six-Layer Crash Protection

Built for warped steel, tipped-up slugs, and worn electrodes.

Every protection layer below runs inside the 10 kHz hardware interrupt on Hardware v2—verified on live flat-sheet and 20-foot 4-axis rotary tables.

2.0 ms Silicon Trigger

Active In-Cut Ohmic Collision Escape

Voltage-only THCs are completely blind when crossing a kerf next to a tipped-up slug or while locked in a corner. A single parallel wire tee off your 24V ohmic shield (J3-2) filters 50 ms pierce blowback and water-table splash, overrides all corner and macro freezes, and triggers an immediate retract in silicon in 2.0 ms up to your Z motor’s limit (150 IPM + +0.040″ / 1.0 mm air gap measured on ClearPath 2311S servos), holding downward Z for 250 ms while the gantry clears the obstacle, and tripping CNC Feedhold if shorted ≥ 150 ms.

0.001″ travel @ 30 IPM • +0.500″ (12.7 mm) lift cap
Zero Macros Needed

Macro-Free Corner, Hole & Kerf Anti-Dive

Reads live 6-axis step speed and locks downward Z automatically whenever deceleration hits 0.08–0.10 G or speed drops < 80% of cruise—even on legacy G-code without M62/M63—waiting 20–25 ms above 88% cruise for the corner puddle to settle. Paired with a motion-compensated 667 V/s (+2.0 V / 3.0 ms) kerf/crater hold up to 600 ms and asymmetric speed limits: full rapid UP, capped 75 IPM DOWN.

Up to 295 IPM UP (ClearPath 2311S) / 75 IPM DOWN • Auto-adapts to FRO
Zero Z Clamps

Zero Software Clamps — Uses Your Full Lifter Stroke

The PCB adds zero ±0.5″ software clamps, letting your torch use every millimeter of your lifter’s physical stroke—whether you have 2″ of travel on a compact lifter or 5+″ on a long-stroke axis. Combines a 4th-order 1 kHz Bessel filter (~0.33 ms flat group delay, zero ringing), adaptive gain scheduling (Kp 90 → 200), and an upward-only slope integrator holding < 0.3 V (< 0.002″) uphill tracking lag on ClearPath 2311S servos, while 3rd-order S-curve jerk limiting keeps open-loop steppers from stalling. At every M5, virtual steps unwind cleanly back to 0 with zero Z DRO drift.

< 0.002″ lag (ClearPath 2311S) • 3rd-order S-curve jerk limited
≤ 20 ms Lock

04 // 4-Axis Rotary Tube Cam Engine (XYZA)

Follows spinning square & rectangular HSS corners before voltage error even develops.

Spinning a bowed 20-foot tube in a 4-jaw chuck makes flat faces rise and corners whip past the torch faster than any voltage-only THC can react. Hardware v2 solves the exact tube geometry and chuck runout directly on the MCU.

Solves Round Pipe, Square Tube & Rectangular HSS in Seconds

01 Analytical Cam + Runout Velocity Feedforward

Differentiates the solved tube wall + bow profile against live A-axis angular speed so Z lifts and drops smoothly around rotating HSS corners before arc voltage error even forms.

02 True Tangential Surface Speed (v_surface(θ))

Computes instantaneous radius r(θ) at 10 kHz and feeds true torch surface speed into the automatic corner-slowdown detector.

03 Corner-Window vs. Kerf Discrimination

Accounts for expected geometric voltage rise on receding HSS corners so corner rolls never false-trigger kerf anti-dive—while keeping flat faces hair-trigger sensitive to slots and bolt holes.

04 Hard +0.50 mm Physical Wall Floor Guard

Enforces a continuous mechanical collision floor +0.50 mm above the rotating, runout-compensated tube surface—physically blocking nozzle-to-wall crashes.

05 // 20 KB Black-Box Flight Recorder & smart-thc CLI

Replace shop-floor guesswork with a 10,000 Hz digital oscilloscope.

Whenever a cut ends or a fault trips, the board freezes 1,024 synchronized 20-byte frames (20 KB) in SRAM—complete with exact 32-bit X/Y/Z_host/Z_thc/A coordinate anchors so you can replay the exact trajectory in seconds.

Zero-Dependency Go Binary

Cross-Platform smart-thc Tooling

Single static binary for macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel), Linux (amd64, arm64, Raspberry Pi armv7), and Windows (.exe). Connects over USB-RS485 or a shared WaveShare RS-485-to-Ethernet TCP bridge.

  • ▸smart-thc dump -ascii-plot: Terminal arc waveform + 46-column timestamped CSV with kerf/corner/ohmic ms breakdown.
  • ▸smart-thc jog up|down <dist>: Unit-aware (mm/in) bench jogging with live cutting interlocks.
