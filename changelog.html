Public Roadmap & Future Milestones

Major capabilities currently in active engineering. Milestones are sequence-ordered by shop priority without delivery dates or calendar commitments. Every item remains unscheduled and subject to real-world cutting feedback.

v1.0 GA
Stable Core, Commercial Shop & Sheet Indexing
Production Target

What 1.0 means: A stable, production-ready core CAM engine across 2D flat plate and 4-axis rotary tube. Individual controller post-processors will continue to carry Beta badges until verified on live customer machines with real-world shop feedback.

01

Commercial Shop Business Suite

Export complete multi-profile assemblies in one zip file, import CSV bills of materials, track remnant drops, and print clean shop travelers with job cost estimates.

02

Sheet Indexing (Oversized DXF Auto-Split)

Available to both Maker and Pro Shop licenses: automatically splits oversized 2D DXF/SVG parts that exceed your plasma table's travel into indexed tiles with registration marks and alignment holes so you can slide the sheet forward and finish cutting.

Post-1.0
Structural, 5-Axis Bevel & Multi-Process Horizon
Future Horizon
01

Auto Tab-and-Slot Weldment Joinery

Cut interlocking self-fixturing tabs and kerf-cleared slots into mating tubes and plates so multi-part assemblies snap together without layout fixtures.

02

Native DSTV (.nc1) Import & Bowed-Stick Probing

Import Tekla, SDS2, and Advance Steel DSTV files directly, and probe warped or twisted mill sticks with multi-station touch-offs to compensate toolpaths in real time.

03

Smart Rapid Collision Avoidance, Plate Skew & Heat Control

Route rapids around cut holes to avoid tipped slugs, dice interior drop-outs and leftover skeletons, align crooked heavy plate with 2-point probing, and spread pierces across thin sheets to prevent warpage.

04

5-Axis Bevel Heads & Tube Weld Gap Control

Full 5-axis V, Y, X, and K bevel cutting for spacing-aware flat plate and variable-dihedral tube copes, plus configurable AWS/ASME weld gap and root-face allowances.

05

Multi-Process & Combo Heads

Dedicated cut charts and toolpaths for oxy-fuel preheat, waterjet corner taper ramping, fiber laser fly-cuts, and plasma-drill/tap combo heads for precision small holes.

06

DXF Auto-Heal, Signage Stencil Mode & Direct CAD Add-Ins

One-click cleanup for messy customer DXFs, PNG/JPG artwork tracing with automatic stencil bridges for floating islands, and 1-click "Send to RotaryCAM" add-ins for Fusion 360, SolidWorks, and Onshape.
