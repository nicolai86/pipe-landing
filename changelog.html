October 29, 2026 v0.3.0
VectorNester 2.0 2D Sheet Nesting • Guaranteed G94 Flat Cutting • 5-Rule THC Filter • Max Amperage Cap

Pack flat brackets and gussets across 2D sheets alongside your tube nests with VectorNester 2.0 (including Part-in-Hole cavity nesting and adaptive guillotine remnant optimization), cut flat stock in guaranteed G94 linear feedrate mode even on G93 rotary machines, switch nesting profiles instantaneously, keep hardware THC active across every flat HSS face, and cap Hypertherm SYNC cartridge selection to your plasma power supply's rated amperage.

  • NEW
    2D XY Sheet Nesting with VectorNester 2.0: Pack flat bar and sheet parts (Profile_Flat) across configurable Sheet Length (X) and Sheet Width (Y) stock using adaptive guillotine remnant optimization, obstacle-relative edge matching, and Part-in-Hole interior cavity nesting with automatic inside-out containment cut ordering so inner parts sever before the outer ring drops. Includes an interactive 2D SVG nest preview showing part contours, interior holes, and 90° rotations, plus exact 3D simulator sheet dimensions.
  • NEW
    Guaranteed G94 Linear Feedrates on Flat Stock: Flat stock always outputs standard G94 (mm/min or IPM) linear feedrates in both single-part and pack mode—even when your active machine profile is set to G93 inverse-time mode for rotary tubes.
  • PERFORMANCE
    Instant Nesting Profile Switching: Switching cutting profiles or editing nesting quantities in Multi-Part Pack Nesting immediately cancels any in-flight G-code compilation and replaces pending UI swaps (hx-sync) so your workspace responds instantaneously.
  • NEW
    Max Plasma Amperage Machine Cap (maxAmperage): Set your plasma power supply's rated limit (45A, 65A, 85A, or 105A) under Machine Settings → THC & Plasma Hardware. Smart cartridge selection caps recommendations to your machine's max amperage, grays out oversized cartridges, and warns you if a selected preset exceeds your power supply.
  • IMPROVEMENT
    5-Rule Hardware THC M63/M62 Filter & Full HSS Sever Unlock: Locks hardware THC (M63 P0) across lead-ins, sharp turns (>10° or R < 15 mm), 4-axis tube corners, slowdowns (<85% feed), and pierce craters, while keeping corner-approach deceleration ramps from double-locking straight flat faces outside the geometric corner window—so hardware THC unlocks (M62 P0) cleanly across all 3 full flat faces on 1″×1″ (25.4×25.4 mm) HSS Startcut and Endcut severs even at high plasma cutting speeds.
  • IMPROVEMENT
    Full-Speed G93 Inter-Feature Indexing, Contiguous Snake A-Axis Cutout Ordering & Optional #1111/#1112 Outputs: Native G93 inverse-time indexing moves between cuts now run at your full machine maxVelA and rapidRate limits instead of the non-G93 Safe Rotation speed (interFeatureRotationFeedRate), automatically disabling Safe Rotation (°/min) in Machine Settings when G93 mode is active. Co-axial internal cutouts at the same X station now cut in a contiguous circular chain and alternate (snake) direction from station to station—eliminating 180° cross-face jumps and cumulative negative A-axis wind-up—and new per-machine Emit Amperage (#1111) and Emit Gas Pressure (#1112) checkboxes under Machine Settings → Dynamic Parameter Modulation (off by default) let you emit pre-pierce RS485 parameters only when your cutter uses them.
  • NEW
    Round & Rectangular Tube Preamble Macros (#1120 Material Width): Add custom startup macros for round pipe (roundPreambleMacro) and square/rectangular HSS (rectangularPreambleMacro) in Machine Settings. Your G-code preamble automatically sets #1120 to the part's width at A=0 before running your ohmic torch-centering routine.
  • IMPROVEMENT
    One-Direction (X) Cut Ordering & Optional Post-M5 Dwell: Built-in profiles now cut sequentially in one direction along X by default so long tubes feed smoothly out of your chuck and rollers without back-and-forth rapids (turn Thermal Hedging back on anytime for thin-wall stock). The post-M5 G4 P200 Z-axis unwind dwell is also now optional and off by default for faster retracts.
  • FIX
    Flat Profile 3D Cut Preview, Profile Override Reset & Single Default Machine: Cutting paths on flat bar and plate parts now sit flat on the 3D surface instead of wrapping around an imaginary cylinder, a ↺ Reset button restores the auto-detected profile after a manual override, and your chosen default machine profile is strictly enforced and loaded on every new STEP file.
October 23, 2026 v0.2.4
Offline MCP Server • Kinematics Physics Tools • Headless CAM

Shipped a standalone, 100% offline Model Context Protocol (MCP) stdio server directly inside release bundles. Fabricators can now automate CAD inspection, zero-beveling, kinematics physics calculations, and G-code generation through Claude Desktop, Cursor, or local LLMs without sending any files to the cloud.

  • NEW
    100% Offline MCP Stdio Server (rotarycam-mcp): Shipped cross-platform binaries for macOS (arm64), Linux (x64), and Windows (x64). Automatically discovers co-located rotarycam.lic and executes 19 CAD, CAM, kinematics, and nesting tools locally.
  • NEW
    Kinematics Physics Tools: Added first-principles mechanical verification tools: axis_max_velocity, axis_max_acceleration, axis_jerk_from_ramp, lead_screw_travel, rack_pinion_travel, and rotary_travel to validate axis drive gearing and stepper/servo limits before cutting.
  • NEW
    Headless STEP CAM Automation: Exposing analyze_step, apply_zero_bevel, and generate_gcode for fully automated script-driven CAM toolpath generation directly from terminal sessions.
  • IMPROVEMENT
    WebGL 3D Handoff (open_in_rotarycam): Terminal and MCP workflows can launch the interactive 3D WebGL viewport in your default browser with a single tool call, instantly loading your STEP solid with colored cut contours.
  • PERFORMANCE
    Zero-Network License Validation: Offline Ed25519 signature checks verify license tokens in under 1 millisecond with zero external network socket requests.

ClearPath Servos • M108 Toggle • Bent Rectangular HSS

Tuned machine motion presets for high-speed Teknic ClearPath servos, defaulted M108 auxiliary velocity off for SimCNC compatibility, improved rectangular HSS spine unrolling, and refined G93 inverse-time simulator timing.

  • NEW
    Tuned Teknic ClearPath Servo Profiles: Added pre-configured acceleration and jerk limits matched to 10:1 planetary rotary chucks and ClearPath CPM-SDSK servos.
  • IMPROVEMENT
    M108 Feed Velocity Toggle Defaulted Off: Auxiliary M108 velocity commands are now optional and defaulted to off to eliminate syntax conflicts on standard SimCNC and Mach4 controllers.
  • IMPROVEMENT
    Continuous-Spine Bent Rectangular HSS Unrolling: Unfolding rectangular tubing across non-square cross-sections preserves uncut living-hinge webs without tearing corner geometry.
  • PERFORMANCE
    Realistic G93 Simulator Timing: 3D toolpath simulation now accurately calculates rotary axis acceleration ramps and G93 inverse-time cycles, matching actual machine cutting times within 2%.
  • IMPROVEMENT
    Non-Resident Session Storage: Disk-backed streaming session storage prevents RAM spikes when processing large assemblies with dozens of complex tubes.
October 20, 2026 v0.2.2
3D Cut-and-Bend Unfolding • Multi-Stick ZIP Export • SimCNC 3.6

Introduced 3D Bent Tube cut-and-bend unfolding for square and rectangular tubing, multi-stick ZIP G-code bundling, dedicated SimCNC 3.5 vs. 3.6 beta post-processor profiles, and flat wall corner tracking.

  • NEW
    3D Bent Tube Cut-and-Bend Unfolding: Import 3D bent tube STEP files. RotaryCAM automatically detects bend centerlines, unfolds the tube into a straight stick, and programs kerf-relief slots with living hinge spines.
  • NEW
    Multi-Stick ZIP Download: Automatically packages all nested sticks into a single ZIP archive containing individual numerical control files (stick_01.nc, stick_02.nc) and a complete nesting manifest.
  • IMPROVEMENT
    SimCNC 3.5 vs. 3.6 Beta Controller Profiles: Tailored G-code output supporting SimCNC 3.6 beta kinematics updates and legacy 3.5 motion scripts.
  • IMPROVEMENT
    Configurable X Travel Direction: Machine profiles now support toggling +X forward vs. -X forward motion to match your rotary chuck orientation (front-mounted vs. rear-mounted).
  • IMPROVEMENT
    Collapsible Bend Groups: Inspect each bend independently, override relief cut counts (e.g. 3 → 5 cuts), and adjust uncut web width (Edge Left: 30 mm) directly in the cut-path panel.
  • FIX
    Flat HSS Cheek Cut Corner Tracking: Fixed lead-in divots on rectangular tube flat walls by forcing straight-line tangent entries on outer corner radiuses.
October 18, 2026 v0.2.1
1D Mill-Stick Nesting • Kerf Compensation • Remnant Management

Shipped universal multi-part stick nesting. Pack full CAD chassis frames and headache racks across standard 20ft / 6m mill sticks with automated kerf spacing, remnant tracking, and thermal hop cut sequencing.

  • NEW
    Universal 1D Stick Nesting: Ingest multi-body assemblies (up to 37 tubes) and pack matching cross-sections across multiple sticks with configurable inter-part gaps (e.g. 10 mm).
  • NEW
    Remnant Management & Cut Efficiency: Computes exact remnant offcut lengths and displays stick utilization percentages (consistently exceeding 99% on production runs).
  • PERFORMANCE
    Thermal Hop Sequencing: Alternates cut contours along opposite ends of the tube to minimize localized heat buildup and eliminate thermal expansion drift during long cutting jobs.
  • IMPROVEMENT
    Automatic Profile Cross-Section Filtering: Automatically separates 2x2 square tubing from 1.75in round tube and routes parts to corresponding stock nests.
October 16, 2026 v0.2.0
Dual-Curve Zero-Bevel • 4D Feedrate Scaling • FOVNet AI

Initial public beta milestone: Dual-curve zero-bevel saddle joint compensation, 4D SimCNC feedrate scaling, and deep neural network solid profile classification from raw STEP B-Rep topology.

  • NEW
    Dual-Curve Zero-Bevel Saddle Compensation: Solves the perpendicular torch problem on round pipe. Shifts cutting curves to the inner cylinder intersection, eliminating grinding for tight, zero-gap saddle fitups.
  • NEW
    4D SimCNC Trajectory Feedrate Scaling: Dynamically calculates 4D Euclidean motion vector scaling (useSimCNC: true) to guarantee steady surface torch velocity across rapid rotary moves without native inverse-time support.
  • NEW
    FOVNet Solid Profile Classifier: Graph neural network analyzes 3D STEP geometry directly, identifying round pipe, square tube, rectangular HSS, angle iron, and channel profiles instantly.
  • NEW
    Dynamic THC Corner Anti-Dive Locking: Issues synchronized M62 / M63 high-speed digital output commands during corner slowdowns, preventing the torch from diving into the tube wall.
