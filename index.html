CORE CAPABILITIES

Engineered for 4-axis rotary plasma. Not rolled 2D sheet metal.

Conventional plasma CAM forces fabricators to manually unwrap tubes into flat 2D DXFs in CAD. RotaryCAM solves the true 3D B-Rep topology and rotary kinematics directly from your solid model.

01 / TOPOLOGY & AI

Direct 3D B-Rep Recognition

Never unroll solid bodies into 2D DXF flats. OpenCASCADE 7.9 and an embedded TorchScript Graph Neural Network (FOVNet) analyze raw STEP 214/242 files, automatically extracting wall thickness from concentric cylinders or anti-parallel planar faces with 50-micron chordal deflection tolerance.

Zero 2D Unwrapping Hole / Slot Stitching
02 / WELD FITMENT

Zero-Bevel Dual-Curve Compensation

Perpendicular torches create interference bevels on angled saddle joints. RotaryCAM calculates inner-cylinder penetration curves (Option B from CAD inner edges, or Option A analytical) to clip toolpaths to the inner wall boundary. Fishmouths fit drop-in flush right off the chuck with zero hand grinding.

Option A & B Logic Zero-Gap Fitup
03 / 4D TRAJECTORY

SimCNC 4D Feedrate Scaling

Standard CNC controllers lack native Inverse Time (G93) or true rotary TCP. SimCNC measures distance in 4D machine units where A-axis degrees are treated linearly. RotaryCAM computes composite 4D displacement dS_machine / dS_cartesian block-by-block, ensuring exact, constant surface speed across combined XYZA motion.

SimCNC 4D Spoofing G94 / G93 Dialects
04 / CHUCK MOTION

ADLS Singularity Damping & Anti-Dive THC

On rectangular HSS corners, angular acceleration demands spike toward infinity. Adaptive Damped Least Squares (ADLS) limits rotary velocity via real-time manipulability metrics. Dynamic THC freeze signals (M62/M63 or SimCNC variables) lock the Z-axis during corner rotations to prevent torch dives.

ADLS Look-Ahead Dynamic Safe Z
05 / ARC PHYSICS

Chirality, 3D Ramps & Zero-Overburn

Mathematically enforces swirl gas chirality: Clockwise (CW) on outer profiles and Counter-Clockwise (CCW) on internal holes, keeping the square edge on the finished part. Simultaneous 3D Hermite ramp lead-ins blow slag away from nozzles, and early torch extinguish (M05 1.5–3mm early) eliminates divots at lead-in overlaps.

Swirl Chirality Early M05 Extinguish
06 / NESTING

Stick Pack Nesting & Thermal Hedging

Pack multi-part assemblies end-to-end on standard 20ft/24ft/6m raw mill sticks in right-to-left parting sequence for maximum chuck grip. Thermal distance hop sequencing (D_thermal) prevents localized heat concentration and tube banana-bowing, while automated micro-tabbing prevents scrap tip-ups.

OFFLINE TCP & LOOK-AHEAD

Eliminate servo stalls, chuck jerking, and corner blowout.

Standard CNC controllers cannot calculate non-linear rotary tool center point (TCP) inverse kinematics on the fly. RotaryCAM handles the heavy math offline: computing Jacobian accelerations, injecting Levenberg-Marquardt singularity damping, and synchronizing THC lock signals directly into the G-code.

  • ✓ SimCNC 4D Euclidean feedrate scaling (dS_machine / dS_cartesian)
  • ✓ ADLS corner look-ahead & rotary chuck jerk damping
  • ✓ Dynamic THC corner freeze (#1080=0 or M62/M63 digital pins)
  • ✓ Zero-overburn early torch extinguish (M05 based on arc lag)
  • ✓ 3D Hermite ramp lead-ins with pierce puddle protection

100% Client-Side WebAssembly & 3D Simulation
