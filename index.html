SHOP NOTE • G21 (MM) VS G20 (INCH) FEEDRATES Read the Full Feedrate Guide (G94 vs G93 vs SimCNC 4D) →

Why does flat-sheet F change in Inches (G20), while rotary G93 F stays identical?

Flipping between Metric (G21) and Imperial (G20) alters feedrate units on flat plate, but behaves differently when spinning a rotary chuck. Here is how your controller interprets each mode:

1. Flat Sheet & 3-Axis Surface-Roller Wrap (G94)
Distance Per Minute (÷ 25.4)

F represents mm/min in G21 and IPM in G20. Switching units divides feedrate by 25.4 (F2540.0 drops to F100.0). On surface rollers, linear motion matches tube surface travel across all pipe diameters automatically; chuck wraps require per-diameter recalibration.

2. 4-Axis Rotary (G93 Inverse Time)
1 / Minutes (F Stays 100% Identical)

F defines inverse time (move duration = 60 / F seconds). Because both cutting speed and distance divide by 25.4, the unit factor cancels out (F1000 takes 0.06s in both systems). Dividing G93 F by 25.4 would crawl 25.4× too slow and burn out your tube.

3. SimCNC Rotary (< 3.6 4D Vector / 3.6+ G93)
Linear Inches + Rotary Degrees

In SimCNC < 3.6, linear axes convert to inches while chuck A remains in degrees, scaled along the 4D vector √(dX²+dY²+dZ²+dA²) to hold constant surface speed. SimCNC 3.6+ supports native G93 inverse-time directly.
