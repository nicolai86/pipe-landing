Built by fabricators tired of 2D flat unwrappers.

Standard plasma CAM programs treat tubes like rolled sheet metal. RotaryCAM parses full 3D B-Rep topology directly from your solid model.

01 / GEOMETRY

No Manual Unrolling

Never waste time converting solid CAD models into flattened 2D DXFs in SolidWorks or Fusion 360 again. Import your 3D STEP assembly directly.

02 / FITMENT

Zero-Bevel Dual-Curve Compensation

Perpendicular torches create interference bevels on angled saddle joints. RotaryCAM calculates inner-cylinder penetration curves so fishmouths fit flush right off the chuck.

03 / MULTI-PROFILE

Structural HSS & Channels

Not just round pipe. Handles square tubing, rectangular structural sections, and open angle iron with automatic corner-radius tracking and dynamic feed throttling.

OFFLINE TCP & LOOK-AHEAD

Eliminate servo stalls and arc blowout on tube corners.

Most DIY and industrial CNC controllers choke on simultaneous multi-axis rotary acceleration because they lack Inverse Time Feedrate (G93). RotaryCAM calculates Jacobian vector accelerations offline, smoothing feed rates before generating a single line of G-code.
