Instant Version Verification, File Storage & Release Notes

Whether you are verifying that your shop table is running the latest CAM engine update, locating your saved machine kinematics and Hypertherm cut charts to transfer them to a new shop PC, or copying system diagnostics for support, the built-in About RotaryCAM dialog and local storage directories keep your shop cutting without interruption.

Step 01

Click ⓘ About in the Top-Right Header Bar

From anywhere in the RotaryCAM workspace, look at the top-right application header bar (next to ⚙ Machines):

  • Click the ⓘ About button in the top-right navigation bar (you can also click the version badge in the bottom-right status bar).
Step 02

Inspect Build Metadata, Release Changelog & Copy Diagnostics

The About RotaryCAM modal opens over the workspace and is divided into three sections:

  • 1. Build Metadata Table (Top): Displays the active Version (v0.3.2), exact Git Commit hash, Build Date (UTC), CI Build Run ID, host Platform (darwin/arm64), Build Mode, and current License Status.
  • 2. What Changed — Recent Highlights (Center): A scrollable, date-stamped engineering changelog detailing newly shipped capabilities—such as EasyScribe Per-Feature Scribe Mode & Sidecar Markings (.markings.json), Batch Feature Selection & Overrides, 2D XY Sheet Nesting (VectorNester 2.0), Guaranteed G94 Flat Cutting, 5-Rule Hardware THC M63/M62 Filter & HSS Sever Flat-Face Unlock, and 100% Offline MCP Stdio Server.
  • 3. Copy Diagnostics (Bottom-Right): Click 📋 Copy Diagnostics to copy a formatted snapshot of your build metadata and environment to the clipboard when reporting an issue or requesting support.
Step 03

System Storage & Local File Locations (macOS, Windows, Linux)

RotaryCAM keeps all machine settings, plasma charts, and project history entirely local on your machine. When you calibrate a 4-axis chuck, fine-tune cut parameters for thin-wall tubing, or nest parts across raw sticks, your data stays on your local solid-state drive—never locked behind cloud servers or lost when the shop Wi-Fi cuts out.

What RotaryCAM Stores on Your System

Here is what each file and folder does during daily shop cutting:

File / Folder Stored Information & Settings Shop Floor Purpose
machines.json 4-Axis Kinematics & Machine Profiles: Rotary chuck gear ratios (e.g. 10:1 or 20:1), maximum motor velocities, acceleration limits, G94/G93 controller dialect, Safe Z clearance, Torch Height Control (THC) M-codes (M62/M63 P1), and zero-bevel defaults. Your rotary table's motion DNA. Back this up to move your table settings to another computer.
presets.json
advanced_settings.json		 Cut Charts & Plasma Presets: Tuned Hypertherm Powermax SYNC cut charts, custom shop material gauges, kerf widths, cutting feedrates, arc voltages, pierce/cut heights, and pierce dwell times. Your dialled-in cut recipes. Transfer this file so you never re-test kerf widths twice.
processed_files.json
bundles/<uuid>/		 CAD Sessions & Processed Assemblies: Recent project history, uploaded 3D STEP models, extracted parts, and compiled multi-stick nesting layouts. Re-opens your active tubes and nests instantly across app restarts without re-importing CAD.
rotarycam.lic Offline License Token: Cryptographically signed Ed25519 offline license key that unlocks Pro features (dual-curve zero-bevel, multi-stick pack nesting, and custom kinematics). Drop in once to unlock RotaryCAM forever on air-gapped shop tables with zero internet access.
auth_store.json Local Auth Store: Local user session tokens and offline rate-limit status. Keeps your signed-in account state active on the shop computer.

✓ How to Back Up & Transfer Profiles to a New Plasma Table PC

When switching shop computers or setting up a second rotary table, you do not have to re-enter gear ratios or re-tune torch cut speeds by hand:

  • 1. Plug in a USB thumb drive: Open the pipe-macos folder on your old PC (see paths above).
  • 2. Copy your tuned files: Copy machines.json (your chuck ratios, motor velocities, and Safe Z limits) and presets.json (your dialled-in kerf widths, feedrates, and torch height voltages).
  • 3. Paste into the new PC: Install RotaryCAM on the new machine, paste both files into its pipe-macos folder, and launch RotaryCAM. All your machine kinematics and plasma charts are ready to cut immediately.
🔑 Offline License Activation (Air-Gapped Shop Tables)

Rotary plasma tables often sit in metal shop buildings with spotty Wi-Fi or on dedicated air-gapped machine computers with zero internet access. RotaryCAM never requires online activation or recurring server pings:

  • Drop-in location: Copy your signed rotarycam.lic license file into the RotaryCAM application support folder (or place it directly in the same folder as the RotaryCAM executable).
  • Immediate unlock: RotaryCAM checks the cryptographic Ed25519 signature locally in under 5 milliseconds on startup, unlocking zero-bevel saddle compensation, multi-stick nesting, and custom kinematics forever without touching the internet.

Current Release Changelog Entries

The built-in changelog view reflects all recent production engine updates. Below is the full transcript of recent engineering releases directly from the application:

🗓️ Oct 31, 2026 (v0.3.2) EasyScribe Per-Feature Scribe Mode & Sidecar Markings (.markings.json), Batch Feature Overrides, 3D Ramp & Zero-Overburn Defaults & SimCNC #1091/#1092
  • EasyScribe Per-Feature Scribe Mode & Sidecar Markings (.markings.json): Switch any individual CAD feature (holes, notches, cutouts, end cuts) or an entire tube or sheet (✏️ Scribe All) between ⚡ Cut (#1114=1) and ✏️ Scribe (#1114=3) directly in the feature table or batch toolbar. Upload .markings.json sidecar files alongside .step models (or in a .zip) and set any layout mark to scribe or cut. When scribing, RotaryCAM traces the exact CAD line with zero kerf offset, no lead-in/out, and corner-tilt damping bypassed so spring-loaded mechanical scribes (like EasyScribe at -3 mm compression) stay perpendicular around HSS and HSS-O corners—sequenced automatically before plasma cuts.
  • Batch Feature Selection & Batch Overrides: Select multiple features at once (Shift-click range selection, Select All, or filter by feature type) to batch-apply enable/disable, Cut/Scribe mode, Zero-Bevel, Corner Loop, 3D Ramp Lead-In, Zero-Overburn, Tabs, and Feedrate overrides in one step.
  • 3D Ramp Lead-In & Corner-Safe Zero-Overburn Smart Defaults: Enabled by default across closed-profile sever cuts (Startcut/Endcut), cutouts, and holes, with automatic post-corner straight buffer protection (max(kerfWidth, 1.5 mm)) after 90° corners and exact straight-segment splitting so early torch-out (M5) never extinguishes mid-corner.
  • Straight-Wall Midpoint Pierce & Small-Tube Cavity Pierce Derating: Pierces at the true geometric midpoint of unsubdivided straight axial cutout walls on rectangular/square HSS tubes (scaling corner and weld-seam exclusion zones on small < 1.5″ tubes so flat-face walls aren't skipped), and automatically derates stationary pierce dwell on narrow hollow tubes (D_cavity < 1.5″ / 38.1 mm, plus 0.25s 12 GA Mild Steel presets) so the plasma jet doesn't blow across the inner cavity and scar the opposite wall.
  • SimCNC m3.py Pre-Pierce Parameters (#1091 & #1092): Emits #1091 (effective stationary pierce delay in seconds) and #1092 (calculated surface cut feedrate) before M3 for dynamic SimCNC torch-on macro control.
🗓️ Oct 30, 2026 (v0.3.1) Full-Speed G93 Indexing, Contiguous Snake A-Axis Cutout Ordering, Optional #1111/#1112 Outputs & 1″×1″ HSS Sever THC Unlock
  • Full-Speed G93 Inter-Feature Indexing: Native G93 inverse-time inter-feature indexing moves now run at full machine maxVelA and rapidRate limits instead of the non-G93 Safe Rotation feedrate (interFeatureRotationFeedRate), and Safe Rotation (°/min) in Machine Settings is automatically disabled when G93 mode is active.
  • Contiguous Snake A-Axis Cutout Ordering: Co-axial internal cutouts at the same X station sequence along a contiguous circular chain and alternate (snake) direction across stations, eliminating 180° cross-face jumps and cumulative negative A-axis wind-up.
  • Optional Pre-Pierce Amperage (#1111) & Gas Pressure (#1112) Outputs: Added per-machine Emit Amperage (#1111) and Emit Gas Pressure (#1112) checkboxes under Machine Settings → Dynamic Parameter Modulation (defaulting to off) so pre-pierce RS485 parameters are only emitted when enabled.
  • 1″×1″ HSS Sever Cut Flat-Face THC Unlock at High Cut Speeds: Corner-approach deceleration ramps on rectangular and square HSS tubes no longer double-lock straight flat faces outside the geometric corner window, keeping hardware THC unlocked (M62 P0) across all 3 full flat faces on 1″×1″ (25.4×25.4 mm) HSS Startcut and Endcut severs even at high plasma cutting speeds.
🗓️ Oct 29, 2026 (v0.3.0) 2D XY Sheet Nesting (VectorNester 2.0), Guaranteed G94 Flat Cutting, Instant Nesting Profile Switching, Max Amperage Cap & HSS Sever THC Unlock
  • 2D XY Sheet Nesting (VectorNester 2.0): Multi-Part Pack Nesting now packs flat bar and sheet parts (Profile_Flat) across configurable Sheet Length (X) and Sheet Width (Y) stock using adaptive guillotine remnant optimization, obstacle-relative edge matching, and Part-in-Hole interior cavity nesting with automatic inside-out containment cut ordering. Includes an interactive 2D SVG sheet layout preview showing part perimeters, interior holes/cutouts, and 90° rotations, plus exact 3D simulator sheet dimensions.
  • Guaranteed G94 Feedrate Mode on Flat Stock: Flat stock always outputs standard G94 units-per-minute feedrates in both single-part and pack mode—even when your active machine profile is configured for G93 rotary inverse-time mode, so linear XY plate cutting always runs in mm/min or IPM.
  • Instant Nesting Profile Switching & In-Flight G-Code Cancellation: Switching cutting profiles or editing nesting quantities in Multi-Part Pack Nesting immediately aborts any in-flight G-code compilation and replaces pending UI swaps (hx-sync) so your workspace responds instantaneously.
  • Flat Profile 3D Cut Contour Projection & Profile Override Reset: Cutting contours, lead-ins, and lead-outs on flat bar and plate parts (Profile_Flat) project directly onto the flat 3D plate surface instead of wrapping around a cylinder, plus a one-click ↺ Reset button on Profile Override restores the originally detected profile.
  • Hardware THC Unlock Across All HSS Sever Cut Flat Faces: Unlocks hardware THC (M62 P0) cleanly across every flat face on rectangular and square HSS sever cuts (Endcut / Startcut, down to 1″×1″ / 25.4×25.4 mm tubes) by preventing 4-axis rotary corner tangency vertices and slowdown/breakout segments from double-padding adjacent straight runs.
  • Max Plasma Amperage Machine Setting (maxAmperage): Set your plasma power supply's rated limit (45A, 65A, 85A, or 105A) under Machine Settings → THC & Plasma Hardware. Automatic Hypertherm SYNC cartridge selection caps recommendations to your machine's maximum amperage, disables oversized cartridges in the session selector, and warns if a selected cartridge exceeds your power supply.
  • Single-Default Machine Profile & Optional Post-M5 Unwind Dwell: Enforces a single default machine profile that automatically applies whenever you load a new STEP file, and makes the post-M5 Z-axis unwind dwell (G4 P200) optional and disabled by default so machines that do not need a post-cut dwell retract immediately.
🗓️ Oct 26, 2026 5-Rule Hardware THC M63/M62 Filter, Per-Profile Preamble Macros & One-Direction Cut Ordering
  • Per-Profile Closed-Stock Preamble Macros & #1120 Material Width: Optional startup preamble macros for Round Tube (roundPreambleMacro) and Rectangular/Square HSS (rectangularPreambleMacro) in Machine Settings that assign #1120 to the material width at A=0 before running your ohmic torch-centering routine.
  • One-Direction (Monotonic X) Cut Ordering by Default: Disabled Thermal Hedging by default across built-in machine profiles so stock feeds in one direction along X out of the chuck and support rollers. You can still turn Thermal Hedging on in the sidebar or Machine Settings for heat-sensitive thin-wall tubing.
  • 5-Rule Hardware THC M63/M62 Post-Processor Filter: Deterministic multi-pass filter over G1 segments that locks THC (M63 P0) on lead-ins/outs, sharp deflections (>10°) or tight radii (R < 15 mm), 4-axis tube corner rotations, feedrate drops (<85% target speed), and pierce crater proximity (≤5 mm), with acceleration/deceleration padding and straight-run hysteresis so THC only unlocks on settled flat runs.
🗓️ Oct 23, 2026 (v0.2.4) 100% Offline MCP Stdio Server, Drive Physics Tools & Bent HSS Preview Alignment
  • 100% Offline Model Context Protocol (MCP) Stdio Server (rotarycam-mcp): Standalone, 100% offline MCP server binary for macOS (arm64), Linux (x64), and Windows (x64) bundled directly with RotaryCAM. Connect Claude Desktop, Cursor, or local open-weights models to configure machines, analyze STEP files, apply zero-bevel compensation, and generate production G-code using your local rotarycam.lic without sending a single byte to the cloud.
  • First-Principles Drive Physics Tools: Calculate pitch diameters, travel per motor revolution, velocity, acceleration, and S-curve jerk directly from gearbox ratios, pinion teeth, and screw pitch (rack_pinion_travel, lead_screw_travel, rotary_travel, axis_max_velocity, axis_max_acceleration, axis_jerk_from_ramp).
  • Bent Rectangular HSS 3D Feature Overlay Alignment: Fixed 3D Part Preview feature overlay projection on bent rectangular/square HSS tubes so Intrados and Extrados cutouts align accurately with the 3D CAD mesh.
🗓️ Oct 22, 2026 Full-Speed HSS Flat Walls, Corner Tracking, Hypertherm SYNC Cut Charts & Feature Toggles
  • Full-Speed Square & Rectangular Tube Flat Walls & Clean Lead-Ins: Torch motion now accelerates smoothly back up to full cutting speed across the flat faces of square and rectangular HSS tubing between corner rotations—preventing overheated walls, wide kerfs, and heavy low-speed dross—while straight flat-face lead-ins are emitted as a single clean move without tiny micro-steps.
  • Smooth HSS Corner Rotations & Clean SimCNC 3.6 G93 Corner Tracking: Fixed a Z-axis height spike on square and rectangular HSS tube corners so the torch glides smoothly around every corner fillet at constant standoff without lifting or jerking mid-corner. Updated the SimCNC 3.6 Beta (G93 Inverse-Time) machine profile to track HSS corners perpendicular to the radius by default, eliminating corner-exit bevel lag and flat-face Y-axis overshoot.
  • Built-In Hypertherm SYNC Rev 4 Cut Charts & Smart Cartridge Recommendation: Built-in factory Hypertherm Powermax 45/65/85/105 SYNC Rev 4 (810500MU) cut chart library covering 552 presets across Standard Best Quality, Standard High Prod, FineCut High Speed, and FineCut Low Speed consumables in both Metric and Imperial units for Mild Steel, Stainless Steel, and Aluminum. Features automatic cartridge amperage recommendation for thin-wall vs. thick-wall stock with out-of-range warnings and 1-click cartridge switching, automatically matching feedrate, kerf width, pierce height, pierce delay, cut height, arc voltage, and gas pressure to the part's detected wall thickness as soon as a STEP file is loaded.
  • Dedicated Cut Charts Manager & Manual Overrides: Dedicated Cut Charts Manager UI (separate from Machine Settings) where operators can add custom material thicknesses, tweak feedrates and kerf widths, or reset to factory Hypertherm tables. Explicit per-session Manual Override in the sidebar with one-click reset back to the thickness-matched chart preset.
  • Selective Feature Enable/Disable in Single Part Inspection: Operators can now check or uncheck individual holes, slots, notches, cutouts, or scribe marks in Single Part Inspection to skip them in the generated G-code (for both single parts and full-stick pack nesting) without modifying the CAD model. Start and end tube sever cuts are permanently locked on so parts always sever cleanly from the mill stick.
  • Configurable Heat-Optimized vs. One-Direction Cut Ordering: Operators can choose between automatic thermal hedging (staggering internal holes and slots across the tube to prevent heat warping) or strict one-direction monotonic cutting from free-end to chuck.
  • Clean Lead-Out M5 Torch-Off & Immediate Safe-Z Retract: M5 torch-off now fires cleanly at the end of the lead-out immediately before lifting to safe Z height, eliminating brief post-M5 motion at cut height.
🗓️ Oct 20, 2026 Cut-and-Bend Unfolding, Flat HSS Projection & Multi-Stick ZIP
  • Simultaneous X/Y/A Pre-Pierce Positioning in G93 Mode: On controllers supporting native G93 inverse-time feedrates (such as SimCNC 3.6), positioning moves between features now travel in X, Y, and A simultaneously in a single coordinated move rather than stopping between separate linear rapid and rotary indexing steps, while preserving split positioning for G94 controllers.
  • Tube Bending Quota & License Support: Added per-file and hourly bend entitlements across guest, signed-in, and licensed shop tiers (3 bends per STEP file for guests, 10 bends/hour for signed-in accounts, and 100 bends by default on customer licenses).
  • 3D Bent Tube “Cut-and-Bend” Unfolding: Automatic spine detection and unfolding of bent round pipes and square/rectangular HSS into straight-stock pie-cut relief patterns with an uncut extrados living hinge.
  • Straight HSS Cheek Cuts & 5-Zone Corner Relief: True-plane projection onto flat HSS walls (projectContourOntoHSSSurface) eliminating wavy bend lines on flat faces, with configurable relief cut counts, spine width, and inner-wall binding clearance.
  • 3D Bent Toolpath Preview & Collapsible Bend Groups: Renders relief toolpaths directly onto the 3D bent tube mesh and groups relief cuts under interactive Bend #N rows.
  • Multi-Stick Nesting .zip Export: Direct 📦 Download ZIP (N Sticks) buttons in the Pack toolbar, Stick Pager, and G-Code header with automatic G-code refresh on nesting edits.
  • SimCNC 3.5 vs 3.6 Beta Profiles & Instant Solid Matching: Dedicated SimCNC 3.5 (G94 + G93 Emulation) and SimCNC 3.6 Beta (G93 Inverse-Time) machine presets with seamless WebGL preservation on profile switch.
  • Configurable X-Axis Travel & Start Direction: Added Workpiece Moves on X (G92 X-L → X0, free-end to chuck) vs Torch Moves on X (G92 X0 → +max(X)) origin mode in machine kinematics, defaulting SimCNC profiles to workpiece-moving X.

  • Persistent Session History: Automatically saves and restores processed STEP models, solid selections, and generated G-code across restarts.

  • 3D Uncut Stock Envelope & Simulator Plunge Fix: Added translucent 3D stock overlays and accurate SimCNC M3 vertical pierce-to-cut plunge in the 3D simulator.
