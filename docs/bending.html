04. Bending HSS & HSS-O Sticks

How to load a 3D bent tube STEP model, automatically unfold its centerline into a straight stick with kerf-bending relief cuts, customize the number of relief cuts (3 → 5) and the uncut spine bridge width (Edge Left: 30 mm), preview the unfolded straight stick in 3D simulation, and download shop-ready G-code.

Cut-and-Bend Kerf Relief Unfolding for HSS & Round Pipe

Want to build roll cages, bumpers, or chassis hoops without an expensive mandrel bender? RotaryCAM's Cut-and-Bend lets you fold bent tubing by hand from straight stock. Drop your 3D bent tube model directly into RotaryCAM. It detects every bend along the tube centerline, unrolls the curved shape onto a straight stick of square HSS or round pipe, and cuts precision pie-slice relief wedges through three walls—leaving an uncut spine along the fourth wall to act as a solid hinge. After cutting, simply pull the bend shut by hand and weld the seams flush with zero grinder fitup.

Relief Cut Control Per-Bend Cut Count & Spine Width (Edge Left)
Supported Stock Square/Rect HSS & Round Pipe (HSS-O)
Step 01

Select HSS Bending [CUT & BEND] from Quick CAD Fixtures

Start from the main RotaryCAM workspace and locate the Quick CAD Fixtures panel on the left sidebar (or drop your own bent-tube .step file into the CAD Ingestion zone).

  • Click HSS Bending [CUT & BEND] at the bottom of the Quick CAD Fixtures list.
  • RotaryCAM loads the 3D bent tube CAD solid, detects all 3 bends along its centerline, and calculates the unfolded straight-stock length (2030.2 mm).
Step 02

Select Bend #1 & Click ▸ Cuts (3) Next to Config

Once loaded, notice the Bent Tube Unfolded • 3 BENDS badge under Cross-Section & Dimensions on the left sidebar (with a Profile Override selector supporting both rectangular Profile_HSS_Rect and Round Tube (HSS-O)).

  • In the bottom feature table, each detected bend is grouped into an interactive collapsible row (Bend #1, Bend #2, Bend #3) showing its bend angle (BEND 32.5°), bend radius (R=145.0mm), axial X span (1384–1466 mm), and spine orientation (180.0° / Spine 0°).
  • Locate Bend #1 (the first bend group) and click the ▸ Cuts (3) button on the right side of the row—right next to ⚙ Bend and Save.
Step 03

Inspect the Expanded Relief Cuts & Bend Parameters

Clicking ▸ Cuts (3) expands the individual relief kerf cutouts belonging to Bend #1 (Bend #1 • Cut 1/3 at 1397.5 mm, Cut 2/3 at 1425.0 mm, and Cut 3/3 at 1452.4 mm):

  • Each individual relief cut can have its own Lead-In Strategy (Tangent Arc), Lead-Out Strategy (Circular), and per-contour settings tuned if needed.
  • Directly on the Bend #1 parent row, you have two key inline geometry controls:
    • Cuts (default 3): The number of wedge-shaped relief kerf cuts subdividing the bend arc.
    • Edge Left (default 13,5 mm): The width of the uncut metal bridge (living hinge spine) left intact on the outside face of the bend so the tube stays in one piece during cutting and folds cleanly along the outer wall.
Step 04

Change Cuts from 3 to 5, Set Edge Left to 30 mm & Open Cut Preview

For a smoother bend radius with smaller per-slot wedge angles—and a wider uncut spine bridge along the back face—let's update Bend #1:

  • Change Cuts from 3 to 5.
  • Change Edge Left from 13,5 to 30,0 mm.
  • Click Save on the right side of the Bend #1 row (confirming Saved ✓). RotaryCAM immediately recalculates the 5 relief kerf slots (Cut 1/5 through Cut 5/5), updates the 3D bent-tube preview in the center viewport, and regenerates the G-code.
  • Next, click the ⚡ 3D Cut Simulation (Cut Preview) tab at the top of the workspace to inspect the unfolded straight stick.
Step 05

Verify the Unfolded Straight Stick in 3D Cut Preview & Click ⬇ Download

In the 3D Cut Simulation viewport, RotaryCAM displays the physical straight tube (2030.2 mm long) exactly as it is chucked on your 4-axis rotary plasma table:

  • Inspect the 5-Cut Kerf Relief Pattern (Center Viewport): Scrub or play the simulation to verify the 5 evenly spaced relief cuts wrapping three faces of the HSS stick while leaving the 30.0 mm uncut spine intact on the top face.
  • Download G-Code (Top-Right Header): Click the green ⬇ Download button in the top-right G-Code Post-Processor bar to save your .nc program (3349 lines, 167.5s estimated cut time) ready for your CNC controller.
✓ Works on Both Square/Rectangular HSS & Round Pipe (HSS-O)

Whether your CAD model uses square/rectangular structural tubing (HSS) or round pipe (HSS-O), RotaryCAM's Cut-and-Bend engine projects the relief cut geometry around the tube walls—with corner relief on square tube and rotary unwrap on round pipe—so thick walls won't pinch or bind when folded shut.
