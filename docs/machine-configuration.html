Why Accurate Machine Configuration Matters

Unlike flat-sheet 2D plasma tables, a 4-axis rotary tube cutter simultaneously coordinates linear axes (X, Y, Z) in millimeters with a rotary chuck axis (A) in degrees. When sweeping around rectangular HSS tube corners or cutting deep pipe saddles, the rotary chuck must rapidly accelerate and decelerate while keeping the plasma arc's surface velocity constant. RotaryCAM uses your table's exact physical motor limits to calculate smooth rotary acceleration, keep chuck motors from jerking or stalling on sharp tube corners, and freeze your Torch Height Control (THC) before the torch slows down.

Step 01

Open the Machine Profiles & Kinematics Configuration

From the main RotaryCAM workspace, locate the CNC Plasma Kinematics summary card at the bottom of the left sidebar. This card displays your currently active rotary ratio, rapid limits, Z clearance, and post-processor dialect at a glance.

  • Click the ⚙ Config button in the CNC Plasma Kinematics panel on the left sidebar, or
  • Click the ⚙ Machines button in the top navigation bar at any time to open the Machine Profiles & Kinematics modal.
Step 02

Configure Accurate Axis Kinematics, Velocity, Acceleration & Safe Z Limits

The first tab, Axis Kinematics & Limits, defines the physical capabilities of your CNC table's linear motors (X, Y, Z) and rotary chuck drive (A). Entering your table's real limits ensures RotaryCAM matches your machine's true motor speeds and never asks your chuck to spin faster than it physically can.

  • Rotary Drive Ratio & Direction: Specify your chuck reduction ratio (e.g., 10:1 Rotary) and whether +A rotation follows right-hand or inverted conventions.
  • Linear Axes (X / Y / Z) Limits: Set the true maximum rapid velocity (mm/min), maximum acceleration (mm/s²), and S-curve jerk (mm/s³) your drives can sustain without losing steps or faulting.
  • Rotary Chuck (A-Axis) Limits: Set the maximum rotational speed (deg/min, e.g., 36000°/min = 100 RPM), angular acceleration (deg/s²), and angular jerk (deg/s³). During rectangular tube corner transitions, chuck rotation is almost always the physical bottleneck—RotaryCAM uses these limits to smooth out corner speeds so your steppers or servos never lose steps.
  • Safe Z Clearance Height: Defines the safe retract envelope (e.g., 25 mm) above the rotating tube's maximum diagonal radius so rapid moves never clip a spinning square/rectangular corner.
  • Chuck Jaw Dead Zone (65 mm), Min Chuck Grip (80 mm) & Roller “No-Cut” Zones: Enter your chuck jaw keep-out distance (chuckJawDeadZoneMm, default 65 mm), minimum chuck clamping grip length (minChuckGripLengthMm, default 80 mm), and any steady-rest or roller keep-out bands. RotaryCAM displays a translucent red no-cut zone and 3D chuck jaws in the CAD viewer and 3D simulator, subtracts the dead zone from raw stick capacity during 1D tube nesting, shows the exact Min Stock to Load (L_cut + L_dead), and warns you before G-code runs if a cut reaches into the chuck jaws.
⚙ Configurable X-Axis Travel & Start Direction (Workpiece Moves vs. Torch Moves)

Plasma rotary tables move in one of two physical ways depending on whether your chuck pushes the pipe or your torch gantry rolls along the bed. Configure your machine's exact kinematic direction in the Axis Kinematics & Limits tab:

  • Workpiece Moves on X (SimCNC Rotary Chuck Default): For rotary tables where the chuck carriage pushes and pulls the tube along the bed beneath a stationary-X plasma torch. RotaryCAM zeroes at the far right-hand free end of the stock using G92 X-Length (e.g. G92 X-6000.00 Y0 Z0 A0). The machine cuts each finished tube from the free end first and feeds backward toward X0 at the chuck face, so finished parts drop cleanly onto the scrap table while the remaining tube stays rigidly chucked.
  • Torch Moves on X (0 to max(X)): For rotary attachments mounted inside a standard cutting table where the stock stays parked in the chuck and the torch gantry rolls down the tube. RotaryCAM sets your stock datum at G92 X0 Y0 Z0 A0 and cuts forward from X = 0 toward +max(X) along the bed.

Selecting the matching travel direction ensures your G-code programs never trip machine limit switches, cut in reverse, or drop stock out of the chuck prematurely.

✓ Multiple Profiles Persisted Across Restarts

You can maintain separate profiles for different machines, rotary chucks, or plasma power sources using the left list (+ New or Clone). All configured profiles are automatically saved and persisted across browser/app restarts.

Step 03

Select Controller Dialect & Post-Processor Compensation

Switch to the Controller & Compensation tab to select your CNC plasma controller post-processor. RotaryCAM includes drop-in, zero-hand-edit post-processors for all major industrial, prosumer, and DIY plasma controllers across both Maker ($199) and Pro Shop ($399) licenses:

  • LinuxCNC QTPlasmaC (.ngc) — Native LinuxCNC 2.9/2.10 plasma post with self-contained (o=0, ...) process parameter comments or M190 material table lookup, M3 $0 S1 plasma spindle codes, active-high M62 P2 THC lock / M63 P2 unlock on pin 2, and G93 rotary inverse-time feeds.
  • Langmuir Systems FireControl (.tap) — Optimized for CrossFire, CrossFire PRO, and CrossFire XR tables. Emits mandatory (v1.6-sc) line 1 sentinels, (PS<feed>) footers, paren-only comments, automatic G38.2/G38.4 IHS touch-off loops, H0/H1 THC toggles, and unwrapped Y-axis rotary chuck cutting in G94.
  • ArtSoft Mach4 (Warp9 ESS / TMC3in1) (.tap) — Selectable dual hardware modes for Warp9 TMC3in1 / WarpRunner (emitting m2020 (...) registers with mandatory G04 P0.1 Lua dwells and motion-synced M62/M63 P# torch/THC control) or Standard ESS / external THC (G31 probe + switch offset + G92 Z + M3/M5) with full G93 rotary feeds.
  • MASSO G3 + DTHC (.nc) — Native T112 M6 plasma mode, automated G200 parameter blocks with millisecond pierce delays (D<ms>), explicit buffered M666 (THC OFF) / M667 F<zFeed> (THC ON) corner anti-dive protection, and G93 rotary motion.
  • ArtSoft Mach3 (.tap) — Clean G64 constant velocity (without syntax-breaking P words), G31 straight probe or G28.1 floating-head reference with switch-offset compensation, high-speed motion-synced M11 P# (THC hold) / M10 P# (THC release) to stop blowout divots, and mandatory G93 rotary inverse-time feedrates.
  • Centroid Acorn CNC12 v5.4+ (.nc) — Automated controller-managed cycle macros including G64 ON plasma smoothing, M65 material profile loading, M61 touch-off + pierce cycle, M62 torch-off + retract cycle, M36/M35 THC lock/unlock, and native G93 rotary feeds.
  • grblHAL (32-bit Plasma + Rotary) (.nc) — Built for 32-bit grblHAL controllers ($376 rotary + Plasma THC plugin) with G38.2 touch-off probing, M3/M5 arc control, G4 P<sec> pierce delays, motion-synchronized M63 P0 (THC lock) / M62 P0 (THC unlock) anti-dive protection, and G93 rotary inverse-time feeds.
  • CNCdrive UCCNC (.nc) — Native UCCNC post supporting UC100/UC300ETH/AXBB-E with G31 touch-off probing, M3/M5 torch control, G04 P<sec> pierce delays, native M206 (THC OFF) / M205 (THC ON) corner locking, G52 pneumatic scribe offsets, and G93 rotary motion.
  • FlashCut CNC Plasma (.nc / .gc) — Engineered for the FlashCut Fabrication Head with automated IHS (M50 plasma ON / M51 plasma OFF), G04 X<sec> pierce delays, G602 (Suspend THC) / G603 (Resume THC) corner protection, and G93 rotary tube feeds.
  • EdingCNC Plasma (.nc) — Clean EdingCNC post for CNC720/CNC760 with G38.2 surface touch-off probing, M3/M5 torch control, G4 P<sec> pierce delays, native M21 (THC OFF) / M20 (THC ON) anti-dive locking, and G93 rotary inverse-time feeds.
  • PlanetCNC TNG Plasma (.nc) — Dedicated post for PlanetCNC Mk3 and TNG software with G38.2 touch-off probing, M3/M5 torch control, G04 P<sec> pierce delays, native M54 P0 (THC OFF) / M54 P1 (THC ON) corner locking, and G93 rotary feeds.
  • ShopSabre WinCNC Plasma (.tap) — Built for ShopSabre plasma tables and SideKick rotary attachments, emitting strict WinCNC [square bracket] comments (zero parentheses or semicolons), M61/M62 controller-owned IHS & torch macros, G04 X<sec> pierce delays, G93 rotary feeds, and M66 program end.
  • CSLab SimCNC 3.5 & 3.6 (.nc) — 4-axis Euclidean trajectory feedrate spoofing for SimCNC 3.5 or pure ANSI/ISO G93 inverse-time feed words (F = 1 / Δt) for SimCNC 3.6, coupled with motion-synced M62/M63 P1 torch fire and M63/M62 P0 THC lock.
  • Generic RS-274 (.nc) — Standard ISO 6983 G-code with decimal seconds dwells (G4 P<sec>) and configurable M-codes for open-source and industrial controllers.
📖 In-Depth Controller Setup Guides Available in Step 06

Each CNC controller handles initial touch-off sensing (IHS), torch height control (THC) anti-dive locking, material charts, and rotary axes differently. For full pinout mappings, macro wiring, and practical shop tips for your exact controller, jump down to Step 06 — Supported CNC Controllers & Post-Processor Setup Guides.

⚠ Critical: Why G93 Inverse-Time Is Mandatory for 4-Axis Rotary Cutting

On a flat plate or fixed-radius round tube, a standard units-per-minute feedrate (G94 F3000) works fine. But when cutting square/rectangular HSS tubing, angle iron, or pipe bevels, the distance from the tube's center to the cutting point changes continuously as the chuck rotates. Standard controller "Rotary Radius Compensation" settings only work for a single fixed circular radius and fail on rectangular corners—causing the machine to violently surge or crawl. In G93 Inverse-Time mode, RotaryCAM calculates the exact physical time (Δt) required for each micro-segment and writes F = 1 / Δt on every line, guaranteeing your plasma torch cuts at the exact surface speed you set in your cut chart, regardless of stock shape or corner radius.

Beneath the dialect selector, configure your Post-Processor Compensation Strategy:

  • Enable Acceleration & Jerk Profiling: Models your machine's real motor acceleration in software so feedrate changes and THC anti-dive freeze points match what happens on the table.
  • Smooth Rotary Corner Motion (Square & Rectangular Tube): Automatically smooths chuck rotation when rolling from flat faces into corner fillets, preventing sudden rotational jerks and motor stalls.
  • Enable Kerf Width Compensation & Kerf-True Tabs: Offsets inside holes (CW) and outside cuts (CCW) by half your plasma kerf width so parts hit dimension right off the table, and adds a full kerf width to micro-joint tab gaps so thin-wall tabs never burn away.
  • Rotary Small-Hole (60%) Bolt Speed Scaling: Automatically slows enclosed bolt holes (≤ 25.4 mm / 1″) on round pipe and square/rectangular tubing to 60% cutting speed, stopping plasma arc-lag cone taper so bolts drop straight through without reaming.
  • Configurable ERW Weld-Seam Pierce Avoidance: Set where your tube's internal ERW mill weld seam faces in the chuck on round pipe or square/rectangular HSS, and pierces automatically shift onto clean parent steel—preventing blowback spatter from thick internal weld flash.
  • Heat-Optimized vs. One-Direction Cut Ordering: Choose between automatic heat staggering (alternating internal holes and slots along the tube to reduce heat bow) or one-direction cutting from the free end toward the chuck.
Step 04

Configure Synchronous THC Lock/Unlock & Plasma Hardware

Select the THC & Plasma Hardware tab. On a 4-axis tube cutter, Torch Height Control (THC) configuration is one of the most important settings in the entire CAM pipeline.

When the torch rolls around a rectangular tube corner or a steep saddle apex, the rotary chuck must slow down to stay within its angular acceleration limit. Whenever plasma cutting speed drops, the plasma arc column widens and arc voltage rises sharply. An unlocked voltage-based THC misinterprets that voltage spike as "torch too high" and drives the torch straight into the steel tube.

  • THC Mode & Lock Command Syntax: Choose how RotaryCAM signals your THC to freeze and unfreeze in G-code—such as synchronous digital output macros (M63 P0 to lock / M62 P0 to unlock without motion planner pauses) or controller-specific M-codes.
  • Dynamic Corner Lock Threshold (%): Automatically locks Z-axis height regulation whenever actual surface velocity drops below the configured percentage (e.g., 80%) of target cutting speed, and unlocks it once full surface speed resumes.
  • Lead-In / Lead-Out THC Lock: Keeps THC locked during pierce, ramp lead-in, and overburn lead-out phases where the plasma arc is stabilizing or crossing an existing kerf.
⚡ Why Synchronous M62/M63 Matters

Standard M64/M65 or custom M-codes on many controllers force an exact-stop motion buffer flush, causing the torch to pause momentarily and blow a divot in the tube wall. Synchronous M62/M63 commands toggle the THC lock pin in lockstep with the next G1 motion block at full lookahead speed.

Step 05

Set Default Zero-Bevel & Corner Loop Parameters and Save Profile

Finally, open the Zero-Bevel & Corner Loops tab to define the default geometric compensation values applied to new contours (which can still be overridden per contour during part inspection):

  • Zero-Bevel Pipe Saddle & Square/Rect HSS Miter Defaults: Set default Fade Angle (90°), Intensity (100%), and weld prep Side Gap (mm) for dual-curve round pipe saddles and heel-only pullback (t · tan(θ)) on square/rectangular HSS miters, plus default ERW weld-seam exclusion zones so pierces stay off internal mill flash.
  • Corner Looping (Sharp Direction Changes): Set the minimum/maximum corner angle range (90°–180°) and loop radius (3 mm) used when injecting rollout corner loops into scrap to keep sharp external corners crisp.
  • Manage & Persist Profiles: Use the bottom-left toolbar (+ New, Clone, ★ Active) to create or switch profiles, then click the green Save Profile button in the top-right corner of the modal.

Controller Setup • LinuxCNC 2.9 / 2.10

1. LinuxCNC QTPlasmaC Configuration

LinuxCNC's native QTPlasmaC module handles initial height sensing and torch voltage regulation through real-time HAL external offsets. RotaryCAM produces native .ngc programs tuned for QTPlasmaC tables:

  • Self-Contained Cut Chart Magic Comments (o=0): RotaryCAM writes a self-contained magic comment at the top of your program: 
    (o=0, na=45A Mild Steel 3mm, ph=3.8, pd=0.25, ch=1.5, fr=2800, kw=1.5, th=1, ca=45, cv=115, pe=0.5, gp=75, cm=1)
    When QTPlasmaC loads this file, its internal G-code filter automatically populates pierce height (ph), pierce delay (pd), cut height (ch), cut feedrate (fr), arc voltage (cv), and gas pressure (gp) directly into the operator interface. You never have to manually re-type cutting numbers at the machine console.
  • Central Material Table Mode (M190): If your shop prefers managing consumables inside QTPlasmaC's built-in material library, enable the Use Controller Material Table toggle in Machine Settings. RotaryCAM will emit M190 P<materialNumber> followed by M66 P3 L3 Q1 to sync with your database.
  • Active-High Inhibit THC Lock Polarity (M62 P2 to Lock / M63 P2 to Unlock): In LinuxCNC QTPlasmaC, digital output pin 2 (motion.digital-out-02) connects to plasmac.thc-disable. This is an active-high disable pin—the exact opposite of traditional relays! Turning pin 2 ON freezes the torch, while turning it OFF allows THC height regulation. RotaryCAM automatically handles this inverted polarity:
    • M62 P2 — Synchronously locks/disables THC during corner deceleration, lead-ins, 60% small bolt holes, and before torch-off.
    • M63 P2 — Synchronously unlocks/enables THC when the torch reaches steady cutting speed on straight runs.
  • Small-Hole True Hole Velocity Scaling (M67 E3 Q60): On bolt holes ≤ 25.4 mm (1"), RotaryCAM emits M67 E3 Q60 to drop feedrate to 60% and lock the THC, restoring full speed with M67 E3 Q0 when entering the scrap lead-out.
  • Corner Collision Prevention on Square/Rectangular Tube (#<keep-z-motion> = 1): By default, QTPlasmaC's G-code loader strips out all Z coordinates so plasmac can own torch height. On 4-axis square or rectangular tubing, stripping Z coordinates would cause your torch to slam directly into rotating tube corners! RotaryCAM detects when you are cutting non-round tubing or open structural profiles and automatically injects #<keep-z-motion> = 1 in the header, forcing QTPlasmaC to preserve programmed Z corner climbing moves.
  • Multi-Tool Spindle Codes: RotaryCAM outputs native QTPlasmaC multi-tool codes: M3 $0 S1 for plasma cutting, M3 $1 S1 for pneumatic air-scribe marking (with X/Y offsets managed in HAL), and M3 $2 S1 followed by a micro-move for stationary center-point dimples.
Controller Setup • CrossFire, CrossFire PRO & CrossFire XR

2. Langmuir Systems FireControl Setup

Langmuir Systems tables (CrossFire, PRO, and XR) run on proprietary GRBL-based motion control managed by the FireControl desktop software. RotaryCAM outputs fully compliant .tap programs that load directly into FireControl without parser warnings:

  • Parser Sentinels & Comment Strictness: FireControl rejects standard G-code headers if parser sentinels are missing. Line 1 must begin with (v1.6-sc), and the final line after M30 must contain (PS<speed>) (e.g. (PS2800) in metric or (PS110) in imperial) to populate FireControl's program speed slider. Furthermore, all comments must use parentheses (...) only—semicolons (;), percent signs (%), and variable hashes (#) will cause FireControl to halt with syntax errors.
  • Automated IHS Touch-Off Loop: Because FireControl does not have an internal automatic canned touch-off cycle, RotaryCAM generates Langmuir's exact dual-speed IHS probing loop before every single pierce: 
    H0
G0 X25.000 Y10.000
G92 Z0.0
G38.2 Z-127.0 F2540.0   (Fast ohmic/switch touch probe down)
G38.4 Z12.7 F508.0      (Slow retract until switch un-trips)
G92 Z0.0
G0 Z0.508               (Compensate for switch springback backlash)
G92 Z0.0
G0 Z3.800               (Retract to Pierce Height)
M3                      (Torch Fire)
G4 P0.400               (Stationary Pierce Delay in Seconds)
G1 Z1.500 F1500.0       (Plunge to Cut Height)
H1                      (THC Active)
  • THC Height Regulation (H0 & H1): FireControl turns THC on and off with standalone H1 and H0 command lines. RotaryCAM automatically places H0 before every touch-off, during lead-ins, around small holes, during sharp direction changes, and before extinguishing the arc with M5.
  • Round-Tube Cutting via Y-Axis Motor Swap: FireControl is fundamentally a 3-axis (X, Y, Z) controller with no native 4th rotary axis (A) or G93 inverse-time support. To cut round tubing on a CrossFire table, fabricators physically unplug the table's Y-axis stepper cable and plug it into a rotary chuck drive calibrated so that 1 mm (or 1") of Y-axis motion equals 1 mm (or 1") of surface circumference on the pipe (Y = R_outer · A_rad). When you select FireControl, RotaryCAM automatically wraps round pipe cuts into linear Y moves in standard G94 feedrate mode.
    ⚠ Round Tubing Only on FireControl Y-Swap

    Because a Y-swap setup replaces lateral torch motion with tube rotation, the torch cannot independently translate across the tube while the pipe rotates. Round pipe saddle fishmouths and holes cut cleanly, but square/rectangular HSS tubing, angle iron, C-channel, and off-crown beveling physically require 4 independent axes (X, Y, Z, A) and cannot be run on a 3-axis Y-swap setup. RotaryCAM will alert you if you attempt to load a non-round profile under the FireControl post.

Controller Setup • ArtSoft Mach4 & Warp9 Ethernet SmoothStepper

3. ArtSoft Mach4 + Warp9 ESS / WarpRunner Setup

Mach4 tables powered by the Warp9 Ethernet SmoothStepper (ESS) and TMC3in1 or WarpRunner breakout boards provide ultra-fast hardware torch height control and real-time synchronized I/O. RotaryCAM outputs clean .tap programs with two selectable hardware sub-modes:

  • Sub-Mode A — Warp9 TMC3in1 / WarpRunner: Outputs native m2020 (...) configuration registers to communicate directly with Warp9's Lua plugin: 
    m2020 (W9_HC_LOAD_DEFAULT_CONFIG_VALUES=1)
m2020 (AD1_DELAY_ENABLED=1)
m2020 (AD1_DELAY_VALUE=0.8)
m2020 (AD2_M62_M63_ENABLED=1)
m2020 (AD3_VELOCITY_ENABLED=1)
m2020 (AD3_VELOCITY_PERCENT=85)
m2020 (TARGET_TIP_VOLTS=115.0)
G04 P0.100
    Shop Tip: RotaryCAM always places a mandatory G04 P0.1 dwell right after m2020 register groups so Mach4's Lua macro queue has time to write the hardware registers before the ESS motion planner starts moving.
  • Sub-Mode B — Standard ESS / External Standalone THC: For tables using a Proma, PriceCNC, or external standalone THC, RotaryCAM emits clean G31 probing with switch-offset compensation and standard M3/M5 or synced M62/M63 pins without any m2020 macros.
  • Synchronous Torch & Anti-Dive 2 (AD2) Control: Traditional M3/M5 commands in Mach4 pause the motion planner queue while Lua executes, leaving ugly dwell marks. With Warp9 synchronized I/O enabled, RotaryCAM emits motion-synchronized M62 P<torchPin> (torch ON) and M63 P<torchPin> (torch OFF), alongside M63 P<thcPin> (lock THC) and M62 P<thcPin> (unlock THC) under Anti-Dive Mode 2 for zero-hesitation cuts.
  • Rotary Feedrates & Dwells: Full 4-axis G93 inverse-time mode on all cutting moves. Dwell commands (G04 P<seconds>) are emitted in decimal seconds matching Mach4's default configuration.
Controller Setup • MASSO G3 & MASSO Touch Plasma

4. MASSO G3 + DTHC Configuration

MASSO G3 is a dedicated hardware CNC controller that uses internal canned plasma routines rather than custom PC macros. RotaryCAM produces native .nc files tailored to MASSO's firmware:

  • Mandatory Plasma Mode Header (T112 M6): MASSO strictly requires T112 M6 in the file header to activate plasma features. Changing away from T112 cancels plasma mode (e.g. MASSO's pneumatic air-scribe tool uses T111 M6).
  • Automated IHS Parameter Block (G200): On flat sheet and round pipe, RotaryCAM emits the complete MASSO plasma parameter block: 
    T112 M6
G200 F600 P3.8 D400 C1.5 V115 A0 O1 S0
M3
    F = touch-off feedrate, P = pierce height, D = pierce delay in milliseconds (D400 = 0.4s), C = cut height, V = target arc volts (V0 activates MASSO Auto-Voltage sampling mode), and O1 selects Ohmic probing. Once set, calling M3 causes MASSO to probe, pierce, and drop to cut height automatically.
  • Critical Shop Tip — Why Explicit M666 / M667 Are Required: MASSO's official documentation notes that the A (anti-dive percentage) parameter inside G200 is not yet implemented in MASSO firmware. If you rely solely on G200, MASSO will dive the torch into the metal when slowing down for small bolt holes or tight corners! RotaryCAM protects your torch by emitting explicit buffered M666 (THC OFF) and M667 F<zFeed> (THC ON) commands before every corner, small hole, and lead-out.
  • 4-Axis Square/Rectangular Tube Override: Because native G200 assumes MASSO owns the Z-axis while cutting, RotaryCAM automatically switches to bare G200 with explicit G38.2 touch-off on square and rectangular HSS tubing. This allows programmed 4D Z corner climbs to run smoothly without the controller overriding torch height.
  • Millisecond Dwells: MASSO is the only major controller that strictly interprets G04 P in milliseconds (G04 P400 = 0.4s). RotaryCAM formats all MASSO dwell words in integer milliseconds.
Controller Setup • ArtSoft Mach3 Plasma & 4-Axis Rotary

5. ArtSoft Mach3 Setup

Tens of thousands of plasma tables across the world still run reliably on ArtSoft Mach3. RotaryCAM's Mach3 post (.tap) eliminates the common pitfalls that cause stuttering, dwell blowouts, and corner dives on legacy tables:

  • Constant Velocity Mode (G64 without P words): Mach3 supports constant velocity blending via G64, but adding a tolerance word (such as G64 P0.1) causes Mach3 to throw a syntax error or misread coordinates. RotaryCAM emits clean G64 with strictly non-nested parentheses comments.
  • Selectable Probing (G31 Straight Probe vs. G28.1 Floating Head): Choose between fast straight ohmic touch-off (G31 Z-100 F1000 + G92 Z0) or floating-head microswitch referencing (G28.1 Z10) with automatic switch-offset compensation entered in your Machine Settings.
  • Stop Blowout Divots with Fast Motion-Synced M11 P# / M10 P#: In Mach3, calling standard M3/M5 or custom VBScript macros (such as M101/M102 or M38/M39) forces the trajectory planner to decelerate to a complete dead stop (0 mm/min) before toggling the pin. Whenever your THC locks on a corner or your torch turns off before a lead-out, an asynchronous macro burns a deep blowout hole in the tube! RotaryCAM solves this by emitting Mach3's high-speed motion-synchronized commands:
    • M11 P<output#> — Switches the output ON at the exact microsecond the next axis move begins (instant THC lock with zero hesitation).
    • M10 P<output#> — Switches the output OFF at the exact microsecond the next axis move begins (instant THC unlock, and early arc extinguish on overburn lead-outs).
  • Mandatory G93 Inverse-Time: Mach3's built-in "Rotation Radius" compensation only works for a constant circular radius at Z=0. On rectangular HSS tubing where the lever arm changes continuously, Mach3 surges and stalls under G94. RotaryCAM's native G93 inverse-time mode solves this completely, keeping surface cutting speed rock solid.

6. Centroid Acorn CNC12 Setup

Centroid Acorn and Acorn Six running CNC12 v5.4+ Plasma utilize high-speed optical encoder feedback and dedicated canned macros for industrial reliability. RotaryCAM generates native .nc programs that drop straight into CNC12:

  • Plasma Smoothing & Material Profiles (G64 ON & M65): Centroid CNC12 uses the syntax G64 ON for trajectory smoothing. In the preamble, RotaryCAM calls M65 to load the operator's active material profile from CNC12's Profile Manager, synchronizing pierce heights and cutting voltages.
  • Controller-Managed Canned Cycles (M61 & M62): Instead of scattered probing and dwell lines, Centroid post-processors call Centroid's compiled macros:
    • M61 (mfunc61.mac) — Automatically performs ohmic/floating touch-off, retracts to pierce height, fires the torch, verifies Arc OK, waits the pierce delay, plunges to cut height, and enables THC (M35).
    • M62 (mfunc62.mac) — Automatically locks THC (M36), shuts off the plasma arc (M5), and retracts the Z-axis to Safe Height.
  • Rectangular Tube Dynamic Corner Safety: On 4-axis square and rectangular tubing where the Z-axis must climb and descend rotating tube corners, RotaryCAM calls M61 to touch off and pierce, then immediately calls M36 (Disable THC) so Centroid's fiber-optic THC does not fight programmed Z corner moves, followed by M62 at the end of the cut.
  • Integrated Marking & Scribe Macros: Centroid CNC12 includes dedicated marking macros: M64 performs an automated low-power plasma center-point dimple cycle, while M39 (extend & apply offset) and M40 (retract) control pneumatic air scribes.
