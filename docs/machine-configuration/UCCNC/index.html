CNCdrive UCCNC Setup

Hardened .nc output for UC100, UC300ETH, UC400ETH, and AXBB-E with ArcOK-safe M205-M212 THC handshakes, Checkbox 212 dwell matching, and G93 rotary feeds.

CONTROLLER GUIDE • UC100, UC300ETH, UC400ETH & AXBB-E (.nc) Extension: .nc IHS Modes: Integrated G31 / Neuron Lite / M31 Dwell: Checkbox 212 (sec or ms) • UC100 ≥ 0.2s
6-Phase Plasma Cut Cycle • CNCdrive UCCNC v1.2115+

Torch Motion, Z-Height & Exact Stage G-Code

G0 Rapid / Motion Probe / Dwell Plasma Arc ON THC Active / Cut
01 Rapid & Rotate
G90 G91.1 G17 G40 G49 G64 Startup (+ optional G20/G21 & M205/M207/M209/M211)
G94 F<feed> Linear positioning feed
G0 X<x> Y<y> A<a> Rapid at Safe Z to pierce

Header prints Checkbox 212 dwell unit, hardware board, and Z accel >= 500 mm/s2 check.

02 IHS Touch-Off
G31 Z<tgt> F<probe> Default Integrated G31 touch probe
G92 Z<Zm - offset> Direct single-line switch offset
M31 / Neuron Lite Legacy M31 (flat/round) or external Z

Direct G92 Z<Zm - offset> avoids extra post-trip bounce on floating heads.

03 Pierce & Dwell
G0 Z<pierceH> Move to pierce height
M205 / M207 / M209 / M211 Arm THC + anti-dive before M3 (never M206)
M3 / G04 P<sec|ms> Fire torch + dwell (>= 0.2s on UC100)
G1 Z<cutH> F<plunge> Plunge to cut height (non-ramp)

M206 is never emitted before M3 so UCCNC's ArcOK wait gate completes cleanly.

04 Cut & THC Control
G93 Inverse-time rotary cut strokes
M212 / M211 Flat/Round anti-dive OFF/ON modulation
0.200 mm -> M206 3D/4D check move then bilateral THC lock

On 3D ramps and 4D profiles, a 0.200 mm initial cut move verifies ArcOK before M206 locks Z.

05 Torch Off & Unlock
M211 (if unlocked) Enable anti-dive before torch off
M5 Extinguish plasma arc
G94 F<feed> Restore G94 feedrate
M205 / M209 / M211 Reset state after M206 bilateral lock

Final cut segment splits cleanly before M5 when direct-Z THC is active.

06 Safe Retract & Unwind
G0 Z<safeZ> Retract to Safe Z clearance
G0 A<360*k> / G92 A0 Modulo unwind between pack pieces
G92.1 / M30 Clear temporary offsets & end program

Pneumatic air scribe uses G52 X<dx> Y<dy> offsets cleared with G52 X0 Y0 before plasma.

IHS Modes

Touch-Off & Hardware Modes (Pick One of Three)

  • Integrated G31 + G92 Z<Zm - SwitchOffset> (Default): Probes with G31 and applies direct switch-offset compensation before retracting to pierce height. Works on flat plate, round pipe, 3D ramps, and 4D HSS.
  • Neuron Lite (XY-Only External Z): Leaves all Z motion and touch-off to the Neuron controller, emitting XY(A) contours and M3/M5 only.
  • Legacy M31 Macro (Flat / Concentric Round Only): Calls UCCNC's built-in M31 probe macro (not supported on 3D ramps or 4D HSS corners).
THC Handshake

ArcOK-Safe M205-M212 THC Handshake & 0.200 mm Check Move

  • Pre-M3 Baseline: Emits M205, M207, M209, and M211 before M3 (never M206 before M3) so UCCNC's ArcOK wait gate clears cleanly.
  • Flat & Concentric Round: Modulates M211 (Anti-Dive ON / Lock) and M212 (Anti-Dive OFF / Unlock) across tight corners and speed drops.
  • 3D Ramps & 4D Profiles: Executes a 0.200 mm check move after pierce before locking THC bilaterally with M206, then resets M205/M209/M211 after M5.
Checklist

UC100 Dwell Floor, Checkbox 212 & Z Acceleration

  • UC100 vs. Ethernet / AXBB-E: Selecting UC100 enforces a 0.2 s / 200 ms minimum pierce dwell floor for USB buffering; UC300ETH, UC400ETH, and AXBB-E allow full short-dwell precision.
  • Checkbox 212 (G04 P Units): Match UCCNC Configuration → General Settings → Checkbox 212 (unchecked = decimal seconds G04 P0.5; checked = integer milliseconds G04 P500).
  • Unit Suppression & Z Accel: Enable Suppress G20/G21 if your profile locks units via Steps/unit. Set Z-axis acceleration to ≥ 500 mm/s² for 4D tube motion.
