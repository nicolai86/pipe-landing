ShopSabre WinCNC + SideKick Setup

Built for ShopSabre plasma tables and SideKick rotary attachments (v3.1.06d+ and legacy), emitting G22 mm / G20 in, [bracket] comments, L37.2 D1/D0 inline THC, and L28.1/L92/M66 framing.

Torch Motion, Z-Height & Exact Stage G-Code

01 Rapid & Rotate
G22 | G20 / G90 G40 [G9 S10] G22 mm or G20 in + M65 + G94
L38.1 T<V> F<f> Z<p> R<c> C<a> Optional v3.1.06d+ cut-parameter block
G0 X<x> Y<y> <A|W|B><ang> Rapid using WINCNC.INI rotary letter

Check WINCNC.INI (A, W, or B) so rotary moves and L28.1 command the right SideKick motor.

02 IHS Touch-Off
M61 (Pattern 1) WinCNC touch-off & pierce (+ G92 Z on 4D)
G31 Z<tgt> F<probe> (Pattern 2) Explicit G31 + G92 Z surface datum

Pattern 2 L38 bypass engages automatically on 3D ramps if L38.1 is off or legacy mode is active.

03 Pierce & Dwell
L37.2 D1 / D0 Set THC latch (or Legacy L37/L36)
M61 (P1) | L38 T0.0 P100 (P2) Canned M61 or direct L38 ignition
G04 X<sec> X-word sec dwell (P1 ramp/forced or P2)
G1 Z<cutH> F<plunge> Pattern 2 plunge to cut height

Pattern 1 suppresses duplicate G04 dwells unless 3D ramp (L38.1 Z==R) or forced G04.

04 Cut & THC Control
G93 / G94 G93 inverse-time on rotary strokes
L37.2 D1 / D0 + inline D1/D0 v3.1.06d+: D1=Disabled, D0=Enabled
L37 / L36 Legacy build THC Off / On

On v3.1.06d+, inline D1/D0 rides directly on motion blocks for mid-cut THC modulation.

05 Torch Off & Unlock
G94 Restore G94 BEFORE M62
M62 WinCNC torch off & Z retract

Clean M62 exit keeps torch off and Z clear before next rapid.

06 Safe Retract & Unwind
G0 Z<safeZ> Retract to Safe Z clearance
L28.1 / G92 <A|W|B>0.000 Modulo 0-360 rotary wrap reset
[L92] / M66 Pack-only L92 + M66 program end

L92 emits in Pack Mode only so single-part jobs auto-restore pre-job G92 cleanly.

Units & SideKick

G22 Metric Units, SideKick Axis Letter & Bracket Comments

  • Units (G22 Metric vs. G20 Imperial, Never G21): WinCNC reads G21 as centimeters (10x scale error). RotaryCAM strictly outputs G22 for millimeters and G20 for inches.
  • SideKick Axis Letter (WINCNC.INI: A, W, or B): Check Help → About (targets v3.1.06d+ by default) and confirm your SideKick axis letter in WINCNC.INI so rotary moves, G93 feeds, and L28.1 wraps match.
  • Strict [square bracket] Comments & Framing: Zero parentheses or semicolons; emits M65 init, optional G9 S10 smoothing, G4 X<sec> dwells, L28.1 wraps, and Pack-only L92 before M66.
THC & Patterns

Continuous THC (L37.2 D#), L38.1 & M61/L38 Patterns

  • v3.1.06d+ Continuous THC (L37.2 D1/D0): Emits L37.2 D1/D0 and inline D1 (Disabled) / D0 (Enabled) on motion lines. Older installs can toggle legacy L37/L36.
  • Decoupled L38.1 Cut-Parameter Injection: Enable L38.1 to push T volts, F feed, S dwell, Z pierce, R cut, L lift, N plunge, and C amps, or disable it to use your WinCNC material table.
  • Pattern 1 (M61) vs. Pattern 2 (L38 Bypass): Pattern 1 runs M61 (with centerline G92 Z on 4D HSS and ramps); Pattern 2 bypasses M61 with explicit G31 + L38 T0.0 P100 and engages automatically on 3D ramps if L38.1 is off or legacy mode is active.
Scribe & Pack

Pneumatic Scribe (G54 Baking) & Pack L92

Pneumatic air-scribe XY offsets bake directly into G54 coordinates with zero G52, G53, or G56 conflicts. In Pack Mode, L92 preserves indexed stick G92 X offsets at program end before M66.
