G93 & Guardrails

Trajectory Blending, Micro-Move Coalescing & Guardrails

  • Blending & G93 Precision: Emits G69 plus metric G64 P0.3 (flat/round) or P0.4 (HSS) even inside G20 inch files (banning G61), and formats G93 F to 4 decimals with a 0.0001 floor.
  • Micro-Move Coalescing & Axial Feathering: Coalesces sub-0.05 mm / 0.02° segments and inserts 0.002 mm (0.0026 mm in G20) axial feathering between pure-XYZ and pure-ABC strokes to prevent SimCNC planner stalls.
  • Dwell, Parameter & Arc Guardrails: Suppresses sub-1 ms dwells (G4 P≥1 ms), limits lines to 1 (...) comment, enforces the #1999 parameter ceiling (with #4061 THC allowlist and G4 P1 sync), formats G20 arcs to 5 decimals (splitting full circles, no G90.1/G91.1), and runs phase-preserving G92 A unwinds (no G92.1).
m3.py & Sync

Macro Handshake (#1110-#1114) & M62/M63 Sync

Before M3 S1, RotaryCAM updates modal registers for thickness (#1110), amps (#1111), gas pressure (#1112), target arc voltage (#1090), pierce delay (#1091), calculated surface feed (#1092), 3D ramp flag (#1113), and process mode (#1114).

For full ExtTrajSyncOut0/1 wiring (M62/M63 P0/P1), direct CSMIO/IP-M Arc OK polling, and ___CONF.py / m3.py / m5.py scripts, see Tutorial 07: SimCNC Motion-Synchronized Torch & 3D Ramp Lead-Ins and Tutorial 11: G94, G93 & G20/G21 Feedrates.

config.xml

One-Click config.xml Import

Upload your SimCNC 3.6 config.xml in the Machines modal (Import SimCNC Config (config.xml)) to auto-fill base units, XYZA velocity/acceleration/jerk limits, and rotary rad/s limits. Any axis, IJK, or MotionKit mismatches link straight to the SimCNC config.xml Import Warnings FAQ below.

XML FAQ

CSLab SimCNC XML Import Warnings FAQ

When you import a CSLab SimCNC XML configuration file into RotaryCAM, any warning flagged in the modal maps directly to one of the checks below:

Unrecognized or Missing <baseUnits>

What it means: Your SimCNC config.xml did not list <baseUnits>, so RotaryCAM defaulted Machine Units to mm.
What to do: Check Machine Units (mm vs. in) in RotaryCAM and confirm it matches your SimCNC Configuration units.

axis_3 (A-Axis) Disabled, Unbound, or Set to linear

What it means: In SimCNC, axis_3 (A) is turned off, has no MotionKit assigned, or is configured as a linear axis instead of angular.
What to do: Flat-sheet cuts work as-is. For 4-axis pipe cutting, open SimCNC (Configuration → Axes), enable axis_3, set its type to angular, bind a MotionKit, and re-import config.xml.

Missing <motionKit_N> or Mismatched Dual-Drive Gantry Limits

What it means: Either a <motionKit_N> block is missing under <device><modules>, or the two motors on a dual-drive gantry have different velocity, acceleration, or jerk limits (which can rack the gantry).
What to do: Copy Max Velocity, Accel, and Jerk from SimCNC (Configuration → Modules) into RotaryCAM manually, and make sure both dual-drive gantry MotionKits use identical limits in SimCNC.

<ijkMode> Not Set to relative

What it means: SimCNC is expecting Absolute IJK arc centers while RotaryCAM outputs Incremental (Relative) IJK arcs (and does not emit G90.1/G91.1 on SimCNC), causing huge unwanted arc loops or soft-limit errors.
What to do: Open SimCNC (Configuration → General) and set Arc IJK Mode to Relative.
