Centroid Acorn CNC12 v5.4+ Setup

Two native .nc regimes for Acorn and AcornSix: hands-off M61/M62 canned macros on 2D plate, and explicit M150 + M36 ATHC hold + G93.1 smoothed inverse-time on 3D ramps and 4D rotary tubing.

CONTROLLER GUIDE • Centroid Acorn & AcornSix • CNC12 v5.4+ (.nc) Extension: .nc Comments: Semicolon ; ONLY (0 parens) Parameters: P220=1 • P225=1
6-Phase Plasma Cut Cycle • Centroid Acorn CNC12 v5.4+

Torch Motion, Z-Height & Exact Stage G-Code

G0 Rapid / Motion Probe / Dwell Plasma Arc ON THC Active / Cut
01 Rapid & Rotate
G64 ON / M65 Plasma smoothing + load CNC12 Profile
M36 ; Hold ATHC Regime B (3D/4D) holds ATHC before motion
G0 X<x> Y<y> A<a> Rapid (zero Z words on Regime A 2D)

Regime B sets G54 centerline Z=0 (optional) and retracts to Safe Z; Regime A emits zero Z words.

02 IHS Touch-Off
M61 (Regime A 2D) CNC12 M61 owns touch-off & pierce
M150 ; Touch-off Regime B calls mfunc150.mac (M115 probe)
G92 Z<datum + offset> Zero Z at surface or centerline

M150 prevents M61 from hijacking your Z datum on 3D ramps and 4D HSS corners.

03 Pierce & Dwell
G0 Z<pierceH> Regime B moves to pierce height
M36 ; Hold ATHC Lock Auto THC before ignition
M3 / G4 P<sec> Fire torch + dwell [0.01, 327.67] s
G1 Z<cutH> F<plunge> Regime B plunge to cut height

Regime A delegates touch-off, pierce, dwell, and cut height drop entirely to M61.

04 Cut & THC Control
G94 (2D) / G93.1 (3D/4D) Smoothed G93.1 inverse-time on rotary
M36 ; Hold ATHC Regime B holds ATHC across 3D/4D cuts
15% Anti-Dive (2D) CNC12 internal voltage anti-dive

Regime B sets explicit Z = zOrigin + currZm + cutHeight; M35 unlocks ATHC when needed.

05 Torch Off & Unlock
M62 (Regime A 2D) CNC12 torch off + retract macro
G94 F<feed> Regime B restores G94 BEFORE M5
M5 Regime B torch off

Restoring G94 before M5 ensures rotary mode never bleeds into retract or next M150 probe.

06 Safe Retract & Unwind
G0 Z<safeZ> Regime B retract (omitted on Regime A)
G0 A<360*k> / G92 A0 Exact 360 deg rotary unwind
G64 OFF / M30 Disable plasma smoothing & end program

Entire file uses semicolon ; comments strictly with zero parentheses anywhere.

Two Regimes

Regime A (2D Flat Plate) vs. Regime B (3D Ramps & 4D Rotary)

  • Regime 1: 2D Flat Plate (G64 ON, M65, M61/M62, Zero Z Words): Emits G64 ON smoothing and M65 to load your CNC12 Material Profile, then calls M61 (probe, pierce, dwell, cut height) and M62 (arc off, retract) with zero Z words and CNC12's 15% Voltage Anti-Dive active.
  • Regime 2: 3D Ramps & 4D Rotary (Z=0 Centerline, M150, M36, G93.1): References tube centerline Z=0 under G54, probes via M150 (mfunc150.mac: M115 + G92 Z), holds ATHC with M36 before M3, cuts rotary strokes in smoothed G93.1 inverse-time, and restores G94 before M5. Outputs standalone G4 P dwells, 360° G92 A0 resets, and semicolon ; comments only.
Checklist

CNC12 v5.4+ Commissioning Checklist

  • License Tier (Plasma Pro / Ultimate): Free/base CNC12 caps files at 50 KB. Rotary HSS and saddle jobs routinely exceed 50 KB, so run CNC12 Plasma Pro or Ultimate.
  • AcornSix for Dual-Y + A: Standard 4-axis Acorn boards run out of motor channels on Dual-Y gantries with a rotary chuck. Use Centroid AcornSix whenever pairing Dual-Y motors with an A axis.
  • mfunc150.mac Setup: Install mfunc150.mac in your CNC12 macro folder strictly on M115 + G92 Z (avoid M38 Laser Interlock or reserved OUTPUT1-5 relays).
  • Onboard Fast Inputs (~5 ms vs. Ether1616 ~100 ms): Wire touch-off and Arc OK to Acorn onboard fast inputs (~5 ms) rather than Ether1616 (~100 ms) so M115 stops immediately on contact.
  • Parameters (P220=1 & P225=1): Set P220=1 and P225=1 in CNC12 Setup Parameters for smoothed G93.1 and plasma macro timing.
Scribing & Dimples

Center-Point Dimples (M64) & Pneumatic Scribe (M39/M40)

M64 triggers CNC12's automated low-power plasma center-point dimple cycle, while M39 (extend and apply offset) and M40 (retract) drive pneumatic air scribes. Scribe offsets bake directly into G54 coordinates when needed.
