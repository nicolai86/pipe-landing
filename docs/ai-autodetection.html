06. Fusion 360 & AI Profile Autodetection

How to model tube and pipe structures in Autodesk Fusion 360 using the Structural Member (SM) plugin so that RotaryCAM automatically recognizes every tube profile, wall thickness, and bend centerline straight out of CAD.

Why Fusion 360 + Structural Member Is Required Today

RotaryCAM automatically inspects 3D CAD solids on STEP import to detect each tube’s cross-section family (Profile_HSS_Rect, Profile_HSS_Round, Profile_Angle, Profile_Channel) along with its outer dimensions and wall thickness.

⚠️
Current Requirement — Autodesk Fusion 360 + Structural Member: Different CAD programs structure solid models with slightly different internal surface math. Today, RotaryCAM’s automatic tube scanner is calibrated specifically for Autodesk Fusion 360 assemblies built with the Structural Member (SM) tool. Follow the 8 steps below to guarantee 100% automatic tube recognition and sizing.

Step 01

Open Autodesk Fusion 360 & Verify the Structural Member Add-In

Launch Autodesk Fusion 360 and create or save a new design document (for example, rotarycam-demo):

  • 1. Verify the Structural Member Toolbar Icons: In the DESIGN → SOLID → CREATE ribbon, confirm that the four blue Structural Member icons (SM Structural Member, Miter, Notch, and Trim/Extend) are visible.
  • 2. Document Units: In the left Browser tree under Document Settings, you can work in millimeters (mm, g) or inches—while selecting standard imperial or metric tube profiles inside the Structural Member library.
Step 02

Click Create Sketch & Select a Reference Plane

Structural Member bodies are swept along 2D or 3D sketch lines and arcs representing the tube centerline:

  • 1. Click Create Sketch: Click the Create Sketch icon at the far left of the SOLID → CREATE toolbar.
  • 2. Choose an Origin Plane: Click one of the primary origin planes (XY, XZ, or YZ) in the 3D viewport to enter Sketch mode.
Step 03

Draw Your Tube Centerline Sketch & Click Finish Sketch

Lay out the centerline wireframe for your frame or bent tube assembly:

  • 1. Draw Straight Segments & Tangent Bend Arcs: Use Line (L) for straight members and Fillet (F) or tangent arcs where a tube will be bent around a centerline radius. In this example, the vertical line on the left represents a straight square HSS post, and the D-shaped loop with two tangent corner radii represents a bent tube that meets the post.
  • 2. Click ✓ Finish Sketch: Click the green Finish Sketch button in the top-right toolbar (or in the Sketch Palette) to return to the 3D Solid workspace.
Step 04

Use the Structural Member Plugin to Populate 3D Tube Profiles

With your centerline sketch complete, use the Structural Member add-in to generate real hollow structural section (HSS) or round pipe solids along each sketch segment and trim their intersections:

  • 1. Launch Structural Member Command (4a): Open CREATE → Structural Member → Structural Member Command (or click the SM shortcut icon directly in the top ribbon). Notice that the same submenu and toolbar also provide Miter Command, Notch Command, and Trim Extend Command for coping and mitering joints between members.
  • 2. Select Sketch Lines & Choose Profile Dimensions (4b): Click a sketch segment in the viewport, then select your standard and cross-section in the STRUCTURAL MEMBER COMMAND dialog—for example, Select Standard: ANSI_AISC, Select Family: HSS, Select Size: 1-1/2 x 1-1/2 (38.1 × 38.1 mm), and Select Thickness: 1/8 (3.17 mm). Click OK.
  • 3. Populate Remaining Segments & Notch/Trim Joints (4c): Repeat for the remaining sketch segments (here using round pipe along the U-shaped loop, creating components SM0001:1 through SM0007:1 in the Browser tree) and use Notch / Trim Extend so the round loop saddles cleanly against the vertical square post.
Step 05

Combine Contiguous Tube Segments (Required Only for Cut-and-Bend Support)

By default, the Structural Member plugin creates a separate component/body for every individual line and arc in your sketch (SM0002:1 through SM0007:1).

  • If you are only cutting straight discrete tubes: Leave each structural member as its own separate body.
  • IF you want RotaryCAM Cut-and-Bend unfolding support: Click Modify → Combine in the top toolbar, select the first segment of the bent tube as the Target Body, select all contiguous bend elbows and straight legs of that same tube as Tool Bodies, set Operation to Join (with New Component and Keep Tools unchecked), and click OK.
💡
Why Combine Is Essential for Bending: Merging the straight legs and tangent elbows of a bent tube into a single continuous solid body tells RotaryCAM’s 3D unfolding solver that those segments are fabricated from one continuous stick of stock with kerf-bending relief cuts (see Tutorial 04: Bending HSS & HSS-O). Do not combine separate tubes that are welded together at joints (keep the vertical square post SM0001:1 separate from the curved round loop).
Step 06

Export a STEP File (.step / .stp) of the Entire Design

You do not need to export parts one by one—RotaryCAM ingests multi-body STEP assemblies directly:

  • 1. Open File → Export: Click the File menu icon in the top-left application bar and select Export...
  • 2. Select STEP Format: In the Export modal, set Type to STEP Files (*.stp *.step).
  • 3. Click Export: Save the .step file of your entire design to disk.
Step 07

Load the Exported STEP File into RotaryCAM

Switch to RotaryCAM (Pipe CAM) and ingest your exported rotarycam-demo.step file:

  • 1. Drop or Select Your STEP File (7a): Drag and drop rotarycam-demo.step into the CAD INGESTION (Drop STEP or ZIP Bundle) box in the left sidebar (or click to browse your filesystem).
  • 2. Click Load & Process CAD (7b): RotaryCAM immediately previews the full 3D assembly in the viewport. Click Load & Process CAD (with ☑ One-Shot enabled in the top bar to automatically run feature extraction, unfolding, and G-code generation).
Step 08

Verify Flawless AI Cross-Section Profile Autodetection

As soon as CAD processing completes, RotaryCAM’s neural network (FOVNet) classifies every solid in the assembly without any manual dimension entry:

  • 1. Cross-Section & Dimensions (AI Badge in Left Sidebar): Inspect the CROSS-SECTION & DIMENSIONS card on the left—RotaryCAM automatically identifies Profile_HSS_Rect, outer dimensions 38.1×38.1 mm (1-1/2″ × 1-1/2″), wall thickness 3.17 mm (1/8″), and stock length 110.0 mm.
  • 2. Automatic Profile Grouping in Multi-Part Pack Nesting: Open the CUTTING PROFILE dropdown in the center workspace—both distinct cross-sections in the assembly (Profile_HSS_Rect (38.1x38.1mm) (1 part) and Profile_HSS_Round (OD 42.2mm) (1 part)) are automatically separated into their own stock-compatible nesting groups, ready for simulation and G-code emission.
