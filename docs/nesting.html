03. Multi-Part Stick Nesting

From Multi-Tube CAD Weldment to Multi-Stick G-Code

Roll cages, chassis frames, handrails, and headache racks pack dozens of tube sizes into one STEP file. Load your assembly once to sort parts by cross-section and filter only what fits the stock in your chuck. Adjacent miters and fishmouth copes flip and roll to interlock end-to-end, optional common-line cuts merge matching ends, and every stick cuts from the free end back toward the chuck so the tube stays rigid.

Profile Grouping Auto Cross-Section Match
Packing Algorithm Interlocking 1D + Common-Line
Cut Sequencing Free-End → Chuck (Rigid Support)
Step 01

Load the Headache Rack [COMPLEX] Multi-Body Fixture

Open the left sidebar's Quick CAD Fixtures panel, or drop in your own multi-body .step file.

  • Click Headache Rack [COMPLEX] in Quick CAD Fixtures.
  • All 37 solids load in seconds with their cross-section sizes, lengths, and cut features ready.
Step 02

Switch to the Multi-Part Pack Nesting (37 PARTS) Workspace

Your full 37-part rack appears in the top-left Entire Model Overview. Switch to nesting to lay parts onto raw sticks:

  • Click the Multi-Part Pack Nesting (37 PARTS) tab at the top of the workspace, or
  • Click the blue Open Pack button in the Multi-Part Pack Nesting card on the left sidebar.
Step 03

Open the CUTTING PROFILE Cross-Section Selector

One STEP file often mixes tube sizes, such as 38.1 × 38.1 mm posts, 25.4 × 25.4 mm louvers, and OD 14.3 mm round bars. Parts group by cross-section so only matching profiles pack onto the same stick.

  • Click CUTTING PROFILE in the nesting header bar (showing Profile_HSS_Rect (38.1x38.1mm) (7 parts)) to switch tube sizes right inside the workspace.
Step 04

Select Profile_HSS_Rect (25.4x25.4mm) (17 parts)

The dropdown lists every tube profile found in your CAD assembly and its exact part count:

  • Profile_HSS_Rect (38.1x38.1mm) (7 parts): 1.5" × 1.5" square HSS frame tubes.
  • Profile_HSS_Rect (25.4x25.4mm) (17 parts): 1.0" × 1.0" square HSS crossbars and louvers.
  • Profile_HSS_Round (OD 14.3mm) (2 parts): round tube inserts.
  • Select Profile_HSS_Rect (25.4x25.4mm) (17 parts) to filter the table and select all 17 matching parts.
Step 05

Inspect Multi-Stick Packing (99.8% Efficiency) & Click ⚡ Compile Pack G-Code

All 17 pieces (7480.3 mm total cut length) pack onto 2 sticks of 6000 mm stock with a 10.0 mm part gap:

  • Stick 1 of 2 Preview Bar: Fits 7 parts (Part 10, Part 9, Part 34, Part 36, Part 4, Part 24, Part 35) totaling 5987 mm / 6000 mm. Only 13 mm remains (99.8% EFFICIENCY). Use ◀ Prev Stick / Next Stick ▶ to inspect Stick 2.
  • Interlocking Miters & Copes (180° Flip + A-Axis Roll): Every tube is tested with 180° end-for-end flips and A-axis rotations. Matching miters and fishmouth copes nest face-to-face instead of leaving triangular scrap wedges.
  • Common-Line Sever Cuts: Check Common-line sever cuts to merge matching 90° cutoffs or complementary miters into one shared on-kerf pierce. You cut cutoff pierces in half and leave zero scrap rings on the table.
  • Chuck Jaw “No-Cut” Dead Zone & Min Stock to Load: Your machine's Chuck Jaw Dead Zone (default 65 mm) subtracts from stick length (L_available = L_stick - L_dead). A red hatched no-cut zone marks the chuck end of the stick bar, 3D chuck jaws show in the simulator, and Min Stock to Load (L_cut + L_dead) keeps your torch clear of the jaws.
  • Auto-Scribe Piece IDs (P1, P2) & Mating Callouts (P1 ↔ P12): Multi-part assemblies assign short IDs (P1, P2, …, P12) matching your part list. Turn on Auto-Scribe Part IDs to mark stick-font labels with EasyScribe (#1114=3) or Plasma Mark (#1114=2 at 15A).
    • Part IDs & Witness Marks: Each part gets its own ID plus weld witness footprints where branch tubes or brackets touch a header (≤ 0.5 mm).
    • Mating Joint Callouts: The mating ID scribes inside each end cut (e.g., P12 on P1) and beside the receiving witness mark (e.g., P1 on P12) for fast weld fit-up.
  • 1D Rotary Stick Strip Controls: Reorder packed tubes along the stick, flip parts 180°, nudge rotary phase ΔA, and adjust per-part gaps right inside the Pack panel.
  • Synced Cut-Path & Marking Settings: Edits to cut-path or marking settings sync between Single-Part and Nesting views.
  • Quantities, Spacing & Table Controls: Adjust Part Spacing (Gap) (10 mm), Stock Tube Length (6000 mm), Auto-tab pack seams, and part quantities with gloved-friendly 24 px checkboxes and +/- buttons.
  • Click ⚡ Compile Pack G-Code at the top of the nesting panel to build your multi-stick 4-axis programs.
Step 06

Simulate Stick 1 / Stick 2 in 3D & Download Per-Stick .nc Programs

After compiling, the 3D Cut Simulation opens and the right-hand G-Code Post-Processor Terminal creates one tab per stick:

  • Switch & Download Stick G-Code (Top-Right Panel): Pick Stick 1: pack_stick_1.nc or Stick 2: pack_stick_2.nc, then click ⬇ Download pack_stick_1.nc.
  • Multi-Stick 3D Simulation (Center Viewport): Use ◀ Stick 1 of 2 ▶ and click ▶ Play to verify the full 6000 mm cut order from the free end (X = -5991.78 mm) back toward the chuck (X = 0 mm).
✓ Why Cuts Run Free-End to Chuck

On a 20ft / 6000 mm tube held in one chuck, cuts start at the farthest free end (Piece 1/7) and move toward the chuck. Each part drops off only after all its holes and end miters are cut, keeping the remaining stock rigidly supported until the last cut.

📦 Download All Sticks in One Archive (.zip)

For multi-stick jobs (pack_stick_1.nc, pack_stick_2.nc, pack_stick_3.nc, etc.), click Download All Sticks (.zip) to grab every stick file together. Copy the .zip straight to your USB drive so you never mix up stock runs.

FLAT PACK

2D Flat-Sheet Pack Options & Remnant Guillotine Crop Cut

Switch Nesting to 2D flat plates (.dxf, .svg, or .flat STEP sheets) for flat-sheet pack controls. Inactive Crop and Chain options stay hidden until you enable them.

  • Open-Path Left / Right / Center Kerf (Maker & Pro Shop): Pick Center (On-Line), Left, or Right kerf offset on open slits, edge trims, and bend reliefs. Offset direction follows your CAD stroke direction.
  • Thick-Plate Edge-Start Piercing (Maker & Pro Shop): Enable Edge-Start (Thick Plate) (default overhang 4.0 mm) on heavy plate (≥ 12 mm / 1/2″). Your torch probes IHS on steel at the start point, retracts to pierce height, shifts off-edge into open air to fire, and locks THC until 6.0 mm onto solid steel.
  • Flared Chain / Bridge Cutting (Pro Shop): Link adjacent parts with Daisy-Chain (Single Pierce) using flared bridges that skip half-shape retracing, or use Micro-Bridge (Connected) (default bridge width 3.0 mm or stock auto-default, max link distance 25.0 mm) for retention tabs. Interior holes always cut before chained contours, obstacle-crossing links are skipped, and Chain/Bridge cutting stays separate from Common-Line cutting.
  • 2D Interactive Sheet Drag, Rotate, Flip & Collision Halos: Drag parts in (ΔX, ΔY), rotate in 15° snaps or free angles, and flip H/V. Live halos flag part-spacing and sheet-edge collisions, and Reset Auto-Nest restores the auto-pack anytime (Maker auto-nests 10 flat / 5 rotary with unlimited manual placement; Pro Shop unlimited auto-nest).
  • Void THC Auto-Lock & Flat Corner Loops: Void lock holds THC off within 0.5 × kerf + 4.0 mm of prior kerf slots and pierce craters so the torch never dives across earlier cuts. On .flat profiles, set your corner loop radius (default 3.0 mm) for sharp square corners.

Tutorial: Cropping & Saving a Clean Sheet Remnant (Pro Shop)

Use Guillotine Crop Cut in Pack Mode to square off leftover sheet drop after your parts finish cutting:

  • 1. Nest parts & open 2D sheet controls: Arrange parts on a flat sheet in Nesting and find the docked 2D sheet controls next to the 2D Pack Part Preview.
  • 2. Pick crop mode & margin: Choose Best Single Cut (Max Remnant), L-Cut (Both X & Y), Vertical (X), or Horizontal (Y) in Guillotine Crop Cut. Margin inputs appear automatically (default kerf + 5.0 mm).
  • 3. Check the 2D Pack Preview SVG: Verify the dashed CROP line(s), off-sheet entry marker, and highlighted Usable Remnant rectangle before compiling.
  • 4. Cut parts first, then crop: All part contours run first, followed by the off-edge guillotine crop with void-crossing THC lock. See Tutorial 10: Flat-Sheet CAM & Remnant Crop for the full workflow.
