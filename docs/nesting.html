From Multi-Tube CAD Weldment to Multi-Stick G-Code

Real-world structural fabrications—such as roll cages, chassis frames, handrails, and headache racks—contain dozens of tubes across several cross-section sizes in a single STEP file. RotaryCAM automatically classifies every solid by cross-section profile, filters compatible parts for the stock currently loaded in your rotary chuck, nests them end-to-end across as many raw stock sticks as required, and sequences cuts from the free end toward the chuck so the tube remains rigidly supported throughout the entire program.

Step 01

Load the Headache Rack [COMPLEX] Multi-Body Fixture

Start from the left sidebar's Quick CAD Fixtures panel (or upload your own multi-body .step assembly).

  • Click Headache Rack [COMPLEX] in the Quick CAD Fixtures list.
  • RotaryCAM parses all 37 individual solids in the assembly, extracting cross-section dimensions, lengths, and cut features in seconds.
Step 02

Switch to the Multi-Part Pack Nesting (37 PARTS) Workspace

Once the 37-part assembly loads, the full rack structure appears in the top-left Entire Model Overview. To nest parts onto raw stock sticks:

  • Click the Multi-Part Pack Nesting (37 PARTS) tab at the top of the center workspace, or
  • Click the blue Open Pack button inside the Multi-Part Pack Nesting card on the left sidebar.
Step 03

Open the CUTTING PROFILE Cross-Section Selector

Because a single STEP assembly often mixes several different tube sizes (e.g., 38.1 × 38.1 mm main frame posts alongside 25.4 × 25.4 mm louvers and OD 14.3 mm round bars), RotaryCAM groups parts by their cross-section profile so only matching tubes are nested onto the same physical stick.

  • Click the CUTTING PROFILE dropdown bar near the top of the nesting workspace (currently showing Profile_HSS_Rect (38.1x38.1mm) (7 parts)).
Step 04

Select Profile_HSS_Rect (25.4x25.4mm) (17 parts)

The dropdown lists every unique cross-section detected in the CAD assembly along with the exact part count for each profile:

  • Profile_HSS_Rect (38.1x38.1mm) (7 parts) — 1.5" × 1.5" square HSS frame tubes
  • Profile_HSS_Rect (25.4x25.4mm) (17 parts) — 1.0" × 1.0" square HSS crossbars & louvers
  • Profile_HSS_Round (OD 14.3mm) (2 parts) — round tube inserts
  • Select Profile_HSS_Rect (25.4x25.4mm) (17 parts) to filter the nesting table and automatically select all 17 matching parts.
Step 05

Inspect Multi-Stick Packing (99.8% Efficiency) & Click ⚡ Compile Pack G-Code

RotaryCAM's 1D nesting solver immediately arranges all 17 pieces (7480.3 mm total cut length) across 2 sticks of 6000 mm raw stock with a 10.0 mm kerf/part spacing gap:

  • Stick 1 of 2 Preview Bar: Packs 7 parts (Part 10, Part 9, Part 34, Part 36, Part 4, Part 24, Part 35) totaling 5987 mm / 6000 mm—leaving only 13 mm of remainder (99.8% EFFICIENCY). Use the ◀ Prev Stick / Next Stick ▶ buttons to inspect Stick 2.
  • Customizable Quantities & Spacing: Adjust Part Spacing (Gap) (10 mm), Stock Tube Length (6000 mm), enable Auto-tab pack seams to keep pieces joined on the stick, or increment part quantities (- / +) in the table below.
  • Click the green ⚡ Compile Pack G-Code button at the top of the nesting panel to generate the multi-stick 4-axis G-code programs.
Step 06

Simulate Stick 1 / Stick 2 in 3D & Download Per-Stick .nc Programs

Once compiled, RotaryCAM automatically switches to the 3D Cut Simulation view and populates the right-hand G-Code Post-Processor Terminal with dedicated tabs for each physical stick:

  • Switch & Download Stick G-Code (Top-Right Panel): Click between Stick 1: pack_stick_1.nc and Stick 2: pack_stick_2.nc, then click ⬇ Download pack_stick_1.nc to save the G-code file for your CNC controller.
  • Multi-Stick 3D Simulation (Center Viewport): Use the ◀ Stick 1 of 2 ▶ selector in the simulation toolbar and click ▶ Play to verify the entire 6000 mm stick cutting sequence—ordered automatically from the free end (X = -5991.78 mm) back toward the rotary chuck (X = 0 mm).
✓ Why RotaryCAM Cuts from Free-End to Chuck

When cutting multiple parts out of a long 20ft / 6000 mm tube held in a single rotary chuck, RotaryCAM always sequences features starting at the farthest free end (Piece 1/7) and works progressively toward the chuck. Each finished part drops off cleanly only after all of its internal cutouts and end miters are cut, keeping the remaining tube rigidly clamped in the chuck right up to the final cut.

📦 One-Click Multi-Stick Export: Download All Sticks (.zip)

When nesting large multi-part assemblies that span across several 20ft / 6000 mm mill sticks, clicking the Download All Sticks (.zip) button bundles every compiled G-code program (pack_stick_1.nc, pack_stick_2.nc, pack_stick_3.nc, etc.) into a single organized archive.

Copy the single .zip to your shop USB drive or machine console to run each stick in sequence without downloading files one-by-one or mixing up stock runs.
