1,025 Built-In Cut Charts Across 3 Torch Families, CSV Interchange & Feature Toggling

Wrong feedrate, arc voltage, pierce delay, or kerf turns good tube into scrap. You get 1,025 built-in cut presets across 3 plasma torch families ready to run: Hypertherm Powermax SYNC (3.2 mm / 0.125" standoff, 564 presets), Hypertherm Duramax / Legacy 30XP–105 (1.5 mm / 0.060" standoff, 269 presets), and Generic Air Plasma (1.5 mm / 0.060" standoff, 192 presets) tuned for Razorweld 45, PrimeWeld Cut60, Everlast PowerPlasma, and Thermal Dynamics Cutmaster. Charts cover Standard Best Quality, Standard High Prod, FineCut High Speed, and FineCut Low Speed in Metric (mm, mm/min, bar) and Imperial (GA / in, IPM, PSI) for Mild Steel, Stainless Steel, and Aluminum.

Drop a 3D STEP tube or flat DXF/SVG file onto the table to pair your wall thickness or plate gauge with your active torch family. Cut speed, arc voltage, pierce height, air pressure, and standoff height (1.5 mm vs 3.2 mm) load automatically. Edit any parameter inline, add custom shop gauges, or share tables across machines with unit-aware CSV Import & Export (⬇ Export CSV / ⬆ Import CSV). Need to skip a bolt hole or slot without editing CAD? Uncheck it in the feature table to update your 3D toolpath, pierce count, and G-code on the fly while sever cuts stay locked.

Step 01

Open the Cut Charts Manager (🔥 Cut Charts)

Open the Cut Charts Manager from any view: 3D assembly, single tube inspection, or stick nesting.

  • Check the top-right header bar next to the ⓘ About button.
  • Click 🔥 Cut Charts.
  • Browse factory presets, search gauges, and edit custom shop charts without unloading your CAD part or resetting toolpaths.
Step 02

Browse & Filter 1,025 Factory Presets Across 3 Plasma Torch Families

Filter 1,025 factory cut presets across 3 plasma torch families to match your torch standoff, voltage curve, and consumables:

  • Hypertherm Powermax SYNC (3.2 mm / 0.125" standoff, 564 presets): For Powermax 45/65/85/105 SYNC torches running single-piece cartridges (810500MU Rev 4, 45A–105A). Covers Standard Best Quality, Standard High Prod, FineCut High Speed, and FineCut Low Speed in Metric (mm, mm/min, bar) and Imperial (GA / in, IPM, PSI). Default family on SimCNC profiles.
  • Hypertherm Duramax / Legacy 30XP–105 (1.5 mm / 0.060" standoff, 269 presets): For Powermax30 XP, 45 XP, 65, 85, and 105 torches running mechanized shielded consumables (30A, 45A, 65A, 85A, 105A + 45A FineCut, ~114–138 V arc voltage). Default on LinuxCNC QTPlasmaC, Mach4, MASSO G3, Mach3, Centroid Acorn, UCCNC, FlashCut, EdingCNC, PlanetCNC, and ShopSabre WinCNC.
  • Generic Air Plasma (1.5 mm / 0.060" standoff, 192 presets): Conservative blowback / non-HF air-plasma charts (30A, 45A, 60A, 80A, ~106–128 V, 72 PSI / 5.0 bar) tuned for Razorweld 45, PrimeWeld Cut60, Everlast PowerPlasma, and Thermal Dynamics Cutmaster. Default on Langmuir FireControl and grblHAL.
  • Material & Amperage Filters: Filter by Mild Steel, Stainless Steel, or Aluminum. The amperage dropdown updates to match your torch's real range ([30, 45, 60, 80] for Generic Air Plasma, [30, 45, 65, 85, 105] for Duramax, and [45, 65, 85, 105] for SYNC).
  • Machine Standoff Lock: Pick your torch family in your machine profile to lock in its cut height (1.5 mm / 0.060" vs 3.2 mm / 0.125") and arc voltage curve. Your torch stays off the tube and out of the air.
Step 03

Edit Parameters Inline or Add Custom Shop Presets

Every plasma table cuts a little differently based on air dryness, ground clamp placement, and gantry stiffness. Tune any preset or save your own shop charts:

  • Inline Editing: Click any cell in a chart row (Cut Speed, Kerf Width, Arc Voltage, or Pierce Delay), type your shop value, and click Save.
  • Add Custom Presets: Click + Add Preset in the top-right header of the modal to save charts for non-standard tube walls, 4130 chromoly, Hardox, or custom consumables.
  • Saved Across Restarts: Your edited rows and custom charts stay saved locally across browser refreshes and app restarts.
Step 04

Import & Export Cut Charts via CSV (⬇ Export CSV / ⬆ Import CSV)

Bring in your torch maker's spreadsheet, back up tuned shop speeds, or share charts across tables with unit-aware CSV Export and Import:

  • Unit-Aware Export (⬇ Export CSV): Click ⬇ Export CSV to download your active table (or filtered view) in your machine's unit system:
    • Metric (mm / G21): Exports thickness_mm, feed_mm_min, kerf_mm, cut_height_mm, pierce_height_mm, pierce_time_s, cut_voltage, gas_pressure_bar plus optional center-mark and peck columns.
    • Imperial (in / G20): Exports thickness_in, feed_ipm, kerf_in, cut_height_in, pierce_height_in, pierce_time_s, cut_voltage, gas_pressure_psi plus optional center-mark and peck columns.
  • Auto-Detected Unit Headers (⬆ Import CSV): Drop in your vendor or Excel CSV. Column headers (_in / _ipm / _psi vs _mm / _mm_min / _bar) are detected automatically, so importing an Imperial IPM chart into a Metric workspace converts every speed, kerf, and height cleanly.
  • Safe Merge on Import: Rows matching an existing (name, source, material, amperage, consumable_type) update in place. New rows append without wiping factory presets, and any new source name (like Razorweld 45 Shop or Fab-Table 2 Tuned) shows up immediately in the Source dropdown.
📋 Sample CSV Headers & Row Format

Edit in Excel, Google Sheets, or any text editor using standard Metric or Imperial headers:

Step 05

Inspect Automatic Wall-Thickness Matching & Standoff Binding

Load a STEP file, and your tube's nominal wall thickness (for example, 3.56 mm / 1/8") is measured right from the inner and outer walls.

  • Look under Cross-Section & Dimensions in the left sidebar for the 3.56mm / 1/8" wall thickness badge.
  • The matching cut chart for your machine's torch family loads automatically: Feedrate (2850 mm/min), Kerf Compensation (1.25 mm), Arc Voltage (128 V), and Pierce Delay (0.4 s).
  • Built-In 1/8″ (3.175 mm) Presets & In-Between Gauges: Standard 1/8″ (3.175 mm) Mild Steel and Stainless Steel structural tube and plate have dedicated factory charts. If your wall falls between standard gauges (like 3.0 mm between 12 GA and 1/8″, or 5.0 mm between 3/16″ and 1/4″), feedrate, voltage, and pierce delay blend smoothly between the nearest charts. You get a clean cut with zero hand math at the table.
  • Manual Override: Want to cut 1/8" tube with 45A FineCut instead of 65A standard? Pick any chart from the dropdown, or click reset to jump back to the auto-matched preset.
Step 06

Toggle Contours in Single Part Inspection

Skip hand-drilled bolt holes, optional slots, or fixture marks without waiting on CAD edits.

  • Scroll to the feature table below the 3D viewport in Single Part Inspection to see each cutout's type, axial position, and size.
  • Enable/Disable Checkboxes: Uncheck any HOLE, SLOT, CUTOUT, or SCRIBE box on the left to drop that cut from your job.
  • Cut, Plasma Mark & Scribe Modes (⚡ Cut / 🔥 Plasma Mark / ✏️ Scribe): Switch any contour (or the whole part via ✏️ Scribe All) between ⚡ Cut (#1114=1), 🔥 Plasma Mark (#1114=2, 15A target or cartridge minimum, 0s pierce delay, exact CAD line without swapping consumables), and ✏️ Scribe (#1114=3 strictly for Coaxial EasyScribe with -3 mm spring compression, arc OFF, and mandatory M0 swap pause, or G52 + M8/M9 for SimCNC pneumatic offset air-scribes). Center-point spotting also supports pneumatic peck center-spotting across all 13 third-party controllers. Mechanical scribes run first, then plasma marks, then through-cuts.
  • One-Click Auto-Scribe Part IDs & Witness Marks: Load a multi-part STEP assembly, and each tube gets a short piece ID (P1, P2, …, P12). Turn on Auto-Scribe Part IDs to stencil stick-font labels on the longest clear flat face or top tube crest, clearing holes, miters, and sidecar art with collinear-merged high-speed witness marking and clean M5 → G0 → M3 S1 hops via EasyScribe (#1114=3), pneumatic air-scribe, or Plasma Mark (#1114=2 at #1111=15A). When branch tubes or brackets meet a header (≤ 0.5 mm gap), weld witness footprints and mating IDs scribe right at each coped end for instant fit-up.
  • Locked Sever Cuts: Tube cutoff cuts (STARTCUT and ENDCUT) stay locked with a badge so you never accidentally leave a part attached to the stick.
Step 07

Verify Omitted Toolpaths & Live G-Code

Uncheck a contour, and your preview, pierce count, and G-code update right away:

  • 3D Toolpath: The omitted cutout, lead-in, and lead-out vanish from the 3D preview immediately.
  • Pierce Count & Cycle Time: Your pierce counter drops right away (for example, from 5 to 4 pierces) and cycle time recalculates, saving air, nozzles, and run time.
  • Live G-Code: The G-code panel on the right strips out the plunge (G00), torch fire (M03), pierce delay (G04), cut move (G01), and arc off (M05) for that contour automatically.
