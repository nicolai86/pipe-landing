02. UI Layout & STEP Cut Path Configuration

Learn how to load a multi-body STEP file, switch between solids in the 3D assembly overview, inspect and customize individual cut contours (STARTCUT, ENDCUT, CUTOUT), apply Zero-Bevel Dual-Curve saddle compensation, and verify the 4-axis toolpath in 3D simulation.

Workspace Layout

Switch machines or toggle mm/in from the top bar anytime. The screen splits into three columns: the Left Sidebar (stock size, plasma settings, 3D Assembly Overview, and STEP upload/fixtures), the Center Workspace (3D Part Preview, inline DXF/SVG Tube Overlay controls, 2D Unwrapped Feature Map, and Feature Table), and the Right Terminal (live G-code). Cut-path and marking edits stay synced across Single-Part and Nesting views.

Step 01

Load the Saddle Joint (Zero-Bevel) CAD Fixture

Drag any single-tube or multi-body .step / .stp assembly onto the CAD Geometry dropzone in the left sidebar. To test a saddle right away without uploading your own file, use Quick CAD Fixtures right below it:

  • Find Quick CAD Fixtures in the left sidebar.
  • Click Saddle Joint (Zero-Bevel) (DUAL-CURVE) to load a two-tube T-joint where a branch pipe (OD 27.0 mm × 3.0 mm wall) copes onto a main chord tube.
Step 02

Pick Your Active Tube in the Model Overview or Solid Bar

Multi-body STEP files split into separate tubes automatically, each tagged with its cross-section (Profile_HSS_Round, Profile_HSS_Rect, etc.) plus inner and outer walls:

  • Entire Model Overview (Top-Left 3D View): Shows the full assembly. Your active tube glows amber/orange (Part 2: solid_2) while other tubes stay steel gray. Click any tube in 3D to switch straight to it.
  • Active CAD Solid Bar (Top of Center Panel): Shows your active part's size (Part 2: solid_2 (OD 27.0mm • L=54.8mm)). You can also step through tubes with the dropdown or ◀ / ▶ arrows.
Step 03

Check the 3D Part Preview & Cut Paths

Below the solid bar, 3D Part Preview & Cutting Contours shows your tube and its color-coded torch path:

  • ● Red (Lead-In): Pierce point and entry sweep from scrap onto the cut line.
  • ● Cyan (Cut Contour): Kerf-compensated 3D path around the tube.
  • ● Green (Lead-Out): Overburn or circular exit at the end of the cut.
  • Click-to-Move Pierces & Tabs (v0.8-C): Click anywhere on the 3D part or 2D unwrapped map to move a pierce point (Move Pierce, Maker & Pro Shop), or click, drag, and delete holding tabs (Edit Tabs, Pro Shop). Lead-ins and lead-outs update automatically in both Single-File and Pack Mode.
  • Orbit, pan, and zoom with your mouse, or snap views with Iso, Side, End, and Top in the top-right corner.
Step 04

Select the ENDCUT Row in the Feature Table

Under the 3D preview and the expandable 2D Unwrapped Stock Feature Map, the feature table lists every cut on your tube: STARTCUT (chuck end), ENDCUT (free-end saddle or miter), and CUTOUT (holes, slots, and copes).

  • Click the ENDCUT row (7E99200A-EBB2-4D59-9B57-AB5D515E73D3 at X = 48.3 mm, A = 267.5°) to highlight that saddle in the 3D viewport.
Step 05

Click ⚙ Config to Tune Lead-Ins, Holes & Bevels

Each row has inline dropdowns for Lead-In, Lead-Out, and Zero-Bevel. Click ⚙ Config in the right-hand ACTION column to open the full 4-column settings drawer for that cut:

  • 1. Lead-In & ERW Weld-Seam Avoidance: Pick Rotational Arc, Tangent Arc, or Linear. Set sweep length (mm) and scrap clearance (mm), or turn on 3D Ramp Lead-In (Flying Pierce) to cut spatter. Pierces also avoid your tube's ERW mill weld seam on round pipe and square/rectangular HSS so you pierce clean steel instead of internal flash.
  • 2. Lead-Out & Small-Hole (60%) Speed: Set overburn (mm) or turn on Zero-Overburn Early Torch-Out (synced M63 shutoff before contour end to prevent exit divots). Enclosed bolt holes (≤ 25.4 mm / 1″) on round and rectangular tubes drop to 60% cut speed automatically. Bolts drop straight through without reaming.
  • 3. Corner Loop, Zero-Bevel & 4-Axis Weld Bevel: Add rollout corner loops or tune Zero-Bevel Compensation (dual-curve pipe saddles and heel-only pullback on HSS miters). You can also enable 4-Axis Round-Tube Eccentric Weld-Prep Beveling (Pro Shop, with 3D preview unlocked free on Beta & Maker).
  • 4. Kerf-Compensated Holding Tabs & Smart Defaults: Leave uncut micro-bridges around a cut to hold slugs or parts in place. Drag or delete them directly on the canvas with Edit Tabs (Pro Shop). Full kerf width is added to every tab gap so thin-wall tabs stay intact, and stock-aware defaults fill lead-in/out, corner loops, tabs, and zero-bevel settings until you override them.
Step 06

Enable Zero-Bevel Compensation (80%), Save, and Open 3D Simulation

On a 4-axis cutter, your torch stays perpendicular to the tube (no 5-axis tilting head). Cut a thick pipe saddle strictly along the outside CAD line and the inside wall will stick out at the flanks, holding the crown open with an ugly weld gap.

  • In Corner Loop & Zero-Bevel, check ✓ Apply Zero-Bevel Compensation and set Intensity (%) to 80% (with Cope / Fishmouth checked).
  • Click Save on the ENDCUT row. It turns Saved ✓ and updates your toolpath immediately.
  • Click ⚡ 3D Cut Simulation in the top bar to watch the compensated 4-axis cut.
🔬 How Zero-Bevel Works on Pipe Saddles & HSS Miters

Round Pipe Saddles & Copes: Reading both your outer and inner CAD wall curves pulls the torch back to the inner wall at the flanks so your saddle drops onto the header pipe with zero gap.

Square & Rectangular HSS Miters & Copes: An angled miter on flat-walled HSS only binds on the obtuse inside heel wall. Zero-Bevel pulls back only that interfering heel wall (t · tan(θ)) and leaves your side walls and acute toes dead flush for zero-grinder fit-up.

🔥 4-Axis Round-Tube Eccentric Weld-Prep Beveling

For V-groove weld prep on round pipe without a 5-axis tilting head, turn on Eccentric Weld-Prep Beveling. Your torch shifts off-center in Y, drops Z to hold standoff, and counter-rotates A across all four quadrants using the pipe's natural slope. 3D preview is unlocked on Public Beta and Maker; G-code export is in Pro Shop ($399) →

🛠️ Bent Tubing: 3D Cut-and-Bend Unfolding

Drop a bent round pipe or square/rectangular HSS model to unfold it onto straight stock with mitered relief slots, dead-straight HSS cheek cuts, inner-wall clearance, and an uncut spine. Read Tutorial 04: Bending HSS & HSS-O Sticks →

Step 07

Run the Interactive 4-Axis 3D Cut Simulation

The 3D Machine Simulation reads your actual right-hand G-code (PROGRAM: G-CODE) and plays back the 4-axis motion in real time:

  • Click ▶ Play in the bottom bar. Scrub the timeline, jump -1s / +1s, toggle ⚡ 10x Rapids, or change speed from 0.25x to 8x.
  • Watch the Live CNC DRO (XYZA) in the top-left corner track X, Y, Z (mm), A (degrees), and live feedrate as your tube spins under the torch.
Step 08

Verify the Completed 4-Axis Toolpath & Export G-Code

At 100% (FINISHED), the full 3D path (rapids, arc lead-ins, kerf- and zero-bevel-compensated cuts, and lead-outs) wraps cleanly around the tube:

  • Check the right-hand G-Code Post-Processor Terminal to verify header notes, stock size, synced M63/M62 THC locks, and G1 X... Y... Z... A... F... blocks.
  • Click the download button in the terminal header to save your .nc file.
