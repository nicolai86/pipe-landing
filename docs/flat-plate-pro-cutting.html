Heavy-Plate Piercing, Open-Path Kerf, Clean Remnants & Zero Torch Dives

Thick plate, open trim cuts, and tight nests punish basic 2D CAM. Blowback wrecks nozzles on 1/2″ steel, crossing an old kerf slot makes THC dive into the slats, and jagged skeletons waste half your sheet. These four primitives protect your consumables and squeeze more usable parts out of every plate.

Void Crash Guard 0.5 × Kerf + 4.0 mm THC Lock
Open & Thick Plate L/R/Center Kerf + Edge-Start
Pro Sheet Pack Guillotine Crop + Chain/Bridge
Step 01

Spatial Void THC Auto-Lock (v0.8-A: Zero Torch Dives)

When a plasma torch crosses a previously cut kerf slot or pierce crater, arc voltage spikes and tricks Torch Height Control into diving the Z-axis straight into the steel.

  • RotaryCAM's SpatialVoidIndex records every cut kerf path and pierce crater automatically across both flat-sheet and rotary jobs.
  • Whenever a later cut, common-line pass, or guillotine crop approaches within 0.5 × kerfWidth + 4.0 mm of an existing void, the planner splits the G1 segment.
  • THC locks automatically through the crossing zone and unlocks only after the torch clears back onto solid metal. Zero manual kerf-crossing rules required.
Step 02

Open-Path Center / Left / Right Kerf & Thick-Plate Edge-Start (Maker & Pro Shop)

Each control sits right inside the Features table where you tune individual contours:

  • Open-Path Kerf (Center (On-Line) / Left / Right): Appears inline automatically in the Features table on unclosed DXF or SVG slit and trim lines (is_centerline_slit). Offset direction stays locked to the original stroke direction so trimmed plate edges hold exact finished size.
  • Edge-Start (Thick Plate) Lead-In: Inside every feature's Lead-In Strategy dropdown. Selecting it reveals the Edge Overhang field (4.0 mm default) and flags an amber tip when cutting plate ≥ 12 mm (1/2″).
  • Safe Off-Plate Ignition: RotaryCAM probes IHS on solid steel at the contour start, retracts to pierce height, shifts off the plate edge by the overhang distance to fire without molten slag blowback, and locks THC until traveling 6.0 mm onto solid steel.
Step 03

Remnant Skeleton Guillotine Crop Cuts (Pro Shop)

Slice clean, square remnants off the back of your plate automatically instead of cutting freehand by eye:

  • In flat-sheet Stock Nesting, open the Guillotine Crop Cut dropdown and pick Best Single Cut, L-Cut (Both X & Y), Vertical (X), or Horizontal (Y).
  • Set the crop clearance margin (kerf + 5.0 mm default) to control how close the shear line runs to your nested parts.
  • Inspect the dashed CROP path(s) and highlighted Usable Remnant area with exact dimensions inside the 2D Pack Part Preview before compiling G-code.
Step 04

Serpentine Daisy-Chain & Micro-Bridge Cutting (Pro Shop)

Cut pierces per sheet or keep lightweight parts tied to the skeleton in one continuous outer pass:

  • Load the built-in Flat Chain & Bridge fixture (flat-chain-bridge-kit.svg) from Quick CAD Fixtures to test on a multi-part layout.
  • Uncheck Common-line cutting first (Chain/Bridge and Common-Line modes are mutually exclusive in flat mode).
  • Select Daisy-Chain (Single Pierce) to link outer contours in a single continuous serpentine runner, or Micro-Bridge (Connected) to keep parts anchored with narrow uncut tabs. Interior bolt holes always cut first before the chained outer pass starts.
