02 • Workflow

Where the Two Workflows Differ

Direct 3D STEP vs. Manual Unwrapping

In a DXF-first CAM workflow, every coped tube or multi-part roll cage must be unwrapped into 2D profiles one by one. In RotaryCAM, you drop the full 3D STEP weldment straight in and keep every part, wall thickness, and profile linked.

Open Structural Shapes

Angle iron, C-channel, and I-beam are tricky to wrap cleanly as flat 2D drawings. RotaryCAM extracts B-Rep faces directly and projects lead-ins safely onto open-profile tangents.

Zero-Bevel Copes on Perpendicular Torches

When a perpendicular torch cuts a tight 45° saddle cope, the inner wall protrudes and blocks fit-up. RotaryCAM calculates inner-cylinder intersection pullback automatically so the mating tube drops right in.

1D Stick & 2D Plate Nesting Included

Pro Shop nests multi-part tube assemblies across 6000 mm sticks with 180° flips, A-rolls, and common-line severs, plus 2D flat-sheet nesting with part-in-cutout packing and guillotine remnant crops.

03 • Honest Fit

When to Choose SheetCAM vs. RotaryCAM

No single CAM fits every table. Pick the tool that matches the parts you actually cut most days of the week.

Best Fit For 2D DXF & Multi-Process

Choose SheetCAM (+ Rotary Plugin) if:

  • You mostly cut 2D sheet metal DXF files and only run round pipe on rare jobs.
  • You rely on custom Lua post-processors written specifically for your table's macros, oxy-fuel preheat, router spindles, or waterjet rules.
  • Your shop already runs SheetCAM on Windows or Linux with dialed-in tool tables and no 3D STEP unwrapping bottleneck.
Best Fit For 3D Tube, Structural & Mixed Shops

Choose RotaryCAM if:

  • You model frames, cages, or trailers in 3D CAD (Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Onshape) and want to cut straight from `.step` assemblies.
  • You cut square/rect HSS, angle iron, channel, or I-beam alongside round pipe and 2D DXF/SVG brackets.
  • You need zero-bevel fishmouth compensation, 3D cut simulation, SimCNC 4D feedrate scaling, or native macOS support with zero subscription fees.
04 • FAQ

Common Fabricator Questions

Can RotaryCAM import 3D STEP files without unwrapping them to DXF first?

Yes. Drop multi-body `.step` or `.stp` assemblies directly onto RotaryCAM. It detects round pipe, square/rectangular HSS, angle iron, C-channel, I-beam, and bent tubes automatically with zero manual unwrapping.

Can I still cut flat 2D DXF and SVG sheet metal parts in RotaryCAM?

Yes. Drop multiple `.dxf`, `.svg`, or `.zip` kits, fix missing drawing scale with magnetic 2-point snapping, set Left/Right/Center kerf on open slits, nest parts inside cutouts, and post native `G20` or `G21` G-code.

Does RotaryCAM work with my existing CNC controller?

Yes. All 14 built-in dialects (SimCNC, LinuxCNC QTPlasmaC, Mach4, Mach3, MASSO G3, Centroid Acorn, Langmuir FireControl, FlashCut, EdingCNC, UCCNC, WinCNC, PlanetCNC TNG, grblHAL, and Generic RS-274 G94) are unlocked in both Maker and Pro Shop.

Do I need an internet connection or monthly subscription?

No. RotaryCAM runs 100% offline on macOS, Windows, and Linux using a single `rotarycam.lic` key file. Maker ($199) and Pro Shop ($399) are perpetual licenses with 1 year of updates included.

Can I run both SheetCAM and RotaryCAM on the same shop PC?

Yes. RotaryCAM outputs standard `.nc`, `.tap`, or `.ngc` files directly for your controller without modifying SheetCAM or any existing post setup.
