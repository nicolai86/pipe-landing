02 • Architecture

Where the Two Products Diverge

Rotary & Structural Included Out of the Box

Instead of bolting separate pipe modules onto a plate nest engine, RotaryCAM parses 3D STEP assemblies natively for round pipe, square/rect HSS, angle iron, C-channel, I-beam, and cut-and-bend tubes.

Hobby-to-Industrial Controller Coverage

Small fab shops and custom table builders rarely run factory Hypertherm CNC consoles. RotaryCAM generates native G93, G94, and SimCNC 4D trajectory G-code for 14 controller dialects right out of the box.

Zero-Bevel Fit-Up on a Standard Torch

Without a 5-axis bevel head, steep saddle intersections leave an overhanging inner edge. RotaryCAM computes the inner-wall intersection curve and clamps the cut path so coped tubes mate tight.

Perpetual Offline Ownership

Activate with a local `rotarycam.lic` file on Mac, Windows, or Linux. There is no mandatory cloud login, no seat-lease timeout if shop Wi-Fi drops, and no recurring subscription required to keep cutting.

03 • Honest Fit

When to Choose ProNest vs. RotaryCAM

Enterprise plate CAM and agile 4-axis tube CAM solve different shop bottlenecks. Choose based on your floor hardware and order volume.

Best Fit For High-Volume Industrial Plate

Choose Hypertherm ProNest / ProNest LT if:

  • You run Hypertherm XPR, MAXPRO, or HPR tables and need certified SureCut True Hole, Rapid Part, and multi-head bevel automation.
  • Your production team relies on ERP/MRP integration, automated job quoting, remnant databases, and multi-shift plate scheduling.
  • Your work is predominantly heavy 2D plate cutting on Windows with an enterprise software budget.
Best Fit For Tube, Structural & Custom Fab Shops

Choose RotaryCAM if:

  • You fabricate handrails, roll cages, trailers, or structural frames from round pipe, HSS, angle iron, channel, or I-beam alongside flat brackets.
  • You run SimCNC, LinuxCNC QTPlasmaC, MASSO, Acorn, Mach3/4, WinCNC, or FireControl and want dialed-in 4-axis rotary + 2D G-code without module upsells.
  • You prefer a fast offline app on macOS, Windows, or Linux with one-time $199/$399 perpetual pricing.
04 • FAQ

Common Fabricator Questions

Does RotaryCAM require a Hypertherm plasma cutter or Hypertherm CNC controller?

No. It ships with 1,025 presets covering Hypertherm SYNC, Hypertherm Duramax, and Generic Air Plasma torches (plus CSV chart import/export), and outputs native G-code for 14 popular hobby-to-industrial controllers.

Can RotaryCAM import multi-part 3D STEP assemblies without extra add-on modules?

Yes. Multi-body `.step`/`.stp` inspection, profile autodetection, cut-and-bend unfolding, and 4-axis kinematics are built directly into the core desktop app.

How does RotaryCAM handle small holes and edge quality compared to industrial CAM?

RotaryCAM applies 60% small-hole speed reduction, synchronous `M62/M63` THC void locking, overburn prevention, kerf-compensated tabs, and zero-bevel fishmouth compensation.

Is RotaryCAM a monthly subscription like ProNest LT?

No. Maker ($199) and Pro Shop ($399) are perpetual offline licenses with 1 year of updates included. Optional renewals ($59/$99/yr) are only needed if you choose to upgrade in Year 2 or later.

Can I test RotaryCAM on my own STEP and DXF files before buying?

Yes. Download the free 30-day offline beta and test your real `.step`, `.dxf`, and `.svg` parts in the 3D simulator with your controller's post output.
