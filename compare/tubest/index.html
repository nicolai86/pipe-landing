02 • Workflow

Where the Two Workflows Differ

Real Plasma Physics, Not Laser Posts

Plasma torches need Initial Height Sensing, pierce heights/delays, Kerf-Crossing THC Auto-Lock over prior cuts, and 60% small-hole feed rules. RotaryCAM bakes those into every cut automatically.

Open G-Code vs. Proprietary .zx

TubesT targets Bochu .zx fiber laser controllers. RotaryCAM posts native G94, G93 inverse-time, and SimCNC 4D trajectory G-code for 16 plasma controller profiles out of the box.

Zero-Bevel for Wider Plasma Kerfs

A 1.5 mm plasma kerf on thick-wall pipe creates real bevel interference on 45° saddles. Zero-bevel pullback and 4-axis eccentric V-bevels solve fit-up before the parts leave the chuck.

Tube Sticks + Flat Sheet in One App

Along with 1D interlocking stick nesting and Cut-and-Bend unfolding, Pro Shop packs 2D DXF/SVG gussets with Matryoshka part-in-hole nesting and Flat-Plate Pro Cuts.

03 • Honest Fit

When to Choose TubesT vs. RotaryCAM

Never force a fiber laser CAM onto a plasma torch or vice versa. Pick the software engineered for your energy source and controller.

Best Fit For Friendess / Bochu Fiber Lasers

Choose TubesT if:

  • You run a fiber laser tube cutter equipped with a Friendess FSCUT, CypTube, or TubePro controller expecting .zx jobs.
  • Your machine uses optical height sensing, laser weld-seam cameras, and multi-bar automatic laser bundle loaders.
  • You cut L/U/T/H profiles exclusively with a fiber laser inside the Bochu ecosystem.
Best Fit For CNC Plasma Tables & Open Controllers

Choose RotaryCAM if:

  • You run a CNC plasma table on SimCNC, LinuxCNC QTPlasmaC, Mach3/4, MASSO G3, Acorn, FireControl, or WinCNC.
  • You need plasma-specific control: IHS touch-off, Kerf-Crossing THC Auto-Lock, True Hole rules, zero-bevel copes, and 1,025 plasma presets.
  • You want tube + flat-sheet CAM offline on macOS, Windows, or Linux with zero dongles for $199/$399 once.
04 • FAQ

Common Fabricator Questions

Can RotaryCAM control ERW tube weld-seam orientation?

Yes. RotaryCAM supports ERW weld-seam avoidance so you can orient critical holes, bends, or copes away from the rolled seam before posting G-code.

Does RotaryCAM support 1D common-line severing like tube laser nesters?

Yes. Pro Shop nests interlocking miters and copes across raw sticks with 180° flips, A-axis rolls, and single-cut common-line severing to cut pierce counts in half.

How does Kerf-Crossing THC Auto-Lock protect my plasma torch?

When a cut path crosses a prior kerf or cutout void, arc voltage spikes and makes standard THC dive into the pipe. RotaryCAM detects every spatial crossing and locks THC automatically until the torch clears the gap.

Does RotaryCAM require a hardware USB dongle?

No. Activation uses a portable offline rotarycam.lic file on macOS, Windows, or Linux with zero USB dongles or cloud checks.

Can I import my own plasma cut charts via CSV?

Yes. Start from any of the 1,025 built-in Hypertherm SYNC, Duramax, and Generic Air Plasma presets or import/export your own unit-aware CSV cut tables.
