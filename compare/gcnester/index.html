03 • Honest Fit

When to Choose GCnester vs. RotaryCAM

Both nest 2D plate cleanly, so the real question is whether you want JD Squared quoting and remnant management on a bundled table, or unified 2D/4-axis CAM across any controller.

Best Fit For JD Squared Owners & Shop Quoting

Choose GCnester if:

  • You are the original owner of a JD Squared table where GCnester is included free out of the box.
  • You want Business-tier remnant inventory tracking and job quoting built directly into your 2D plate nester.
  • You only cut 2D flat sheet on Windows and do not need 4-axis rotary tube CAM.
Best Fit For Multi-Controller, Tube + Plate & Perpetual Shops

Choose RotaryCAM if:

  • You want 2D Matryoshka sheet nesting AND 4-axis rotary tube CAM in one app without paying for separate tube and plate tools.
  • You run SimCNC, MASSO, Acorn, Mach3/4, QTPlasmaC, FireControl, FlashCut, or WinCNC instead of a bundled OEM setup.
  • You want a $199/$399 perpetual offline license on macOS, Windows, or Linux with THC Auto-Lock, 1,025 cut charts, and Flat-Plate Pro Cuts.
04 • FAQ

Common Fabricator Questions

Can I import a batch of DXF or SVG files and nest them together?

Yes. Drop multiple .dxf, .svg, or .zip kits onto the canvas, fix unknown drawing units with 2-point magnetic scaling, set quantities, and pack them onto your sheet.

Does RotaryCAM support common-line cutting and chain/bridge cuts on 2D sheets?

Yes. You can enable 2D common-line sharing, chain/bridge cutting between neighboring parts, open-path L/R kerf for scribe or slit lines, edge pierce, and guillotine skeleton cropping.

How does RotaryCAM handle small bolt holes on flat plate?

True Hole rules automatically slow feedrate to 60%, lock THC during the hole, and adjust lead-in/overburn geometry for clean, round bolt holes.

Can I output Imperial (G20) inches as well as Metric (G21) G-code?

Yes. Toggle between Imperial (G20 4-decimal inches, IPM) and Metric (G21 mm, mm/min) for any controller profile.

Is 4-axis rotary tube CAM included in the same license as 2D sheet nesting?

Yes. Both $199 Maker and $399 Pro Shop keys include full 2D DXF/SVG nesting and 4-axis rotary tube/structural CAM in the same offline app.
