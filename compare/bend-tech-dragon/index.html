02 • Workflow

Where the Two Workflows Differ

Bring Your Own 4-Axis Table

Build a $5k-$15k rotary table with our open hardware blueprint or retrofit your existing plate table with a chuck. RotaryCAM generates tuned 4-axis G-code without locking you into a $40k-$80k+ OEM machine.

Tube + I-Beam + Flat Plate Unified

Dragon is strictly a tube bed. RotaryCAM handles all 6 profiles, adding 4-axis I-beam beamline emulation and full 2D DXF/SVG flat-sheet nesting with guillotine skeleton crops in the same app.

Cut-and-Bend & Zero-Bevel Copes

Unfold 3D bent tube STEP parts into straight Cut-and-Bend relief patterns, or cut tight 45° saddle copes with dual-curve zero-bevel pullback and 4-axis eccentric V-bevels.

1,025 Plasma Charts & THC Auto-Lock

Includes 1,025 factory torch presets (Hypertherm SYNC, Duramax, Generic Air Plasma), spatial Kerf-Crossing THC Auto-Lock, and True Hole small-feature rules out of the box.

03 • Honest Fit

When to Choose Bend-Tech Dragon vs. RotaryCAM

This decision comes down to whether you are buying a complete turnkey production machine or software for your own 4-axis plasma hardware.

Best Fit For Turnkey Tube Production Hardware

Choose Bend-Tech Dragon if:

  • You want a turnkey $40k-$80k+ machine delivered ready to run with factory support, laser alignment, and pneumatic tube engraving hardware.
  • Your shop already lives inside Bend-Tech designing roll cages, chassis, and mandrel-bent assemblies tied to bender tooling.
  • You want single-vendor hardware and software with no machine assembly or controller tuning.
Best Fit For Custom, DIY & Multi-Profile Tables

Choose RotaryCAM if:

  • You already own or are building a 4-axis rotary table running SimCNC, MASSO G3, Acorn, Mach3/4, LinuxCNC QTPlasmaC, or FireControl.
  • You cut I-beams and 2D flat-plate gussets alongside tubes and want 1D stick + 2D Matryoshka nesting in one app.
  • You want $199/$399 perpetual offline software on macOS, Windows, or Linux with zero machine vendor lock-in.
04 • FAQ

Common Fabricator Questions

Can RotaryCAM output bend marks and part numbers without a pneumatic engraver?

Yes. RotaryCAM generates plasma part-ID stenciling, assembly witness marks, and DXF TEXT single-stroke scribing posts that run with either your plasma torch at low current or a dedicated air-scribe output.

Can I cut both structural steel and flat sheet brackets in RotaryCAM?

Yes. All 6 profiles are built in: round pipe, square/rect HSS, angle iron, C-channel, I-beam with 4-axis beamline emulation, and 2D flat sheet DXF/SVG nesting.

Does RotaryCAM support Cut-and-Bend relief slotting on square and round tube?

Yes. Load a 3D bent tube STEP file and RotaryCAM unfolds the bend centerlines into a straight stick with customizable kerf relief slots and spine widths.

What if I build my own rotary plasma table?

Check out our 6-Meter Open Hardware Build Guide and 10 kHz Hardware THC, then pair the table with RotaryCAM's 16 built-in controller profiles.

How much does RotaryCAM cost?

Test free for 30 days offline. Perpetual offline licenses are $199 (Maker) or $399 (Pro Shop) with 1 year of updates included.

Test Your Own STEP and DXF Parts Free for 30 Days

Run RotaryCAM offline on macOS, Windows, or Linux. Drop a tube frame STEP assembly or bracket DXF kit in and verify every 4-axis move on your controller before cutting metal.
