02 • Workflow

Where the Two Workflows Differ

Zero Hardware or OEM Lock-In

Run any DIY, retrofit, or commercial 4-axis rotary table. RotaryCAM ships with 16 built-in profiles covering SimCNC, Mach3/4, MASSO G3, Acorn, QTPlasmaC, FireControl, FlashCut, WinCNC, and grblHAL.

Transparent Perpetual Licensing

No machine-serial restrictions or secondary-owner subscription plans. Buy a $199 Maker or $399 Pro Shop offline key once and install natively on macOS, Windows, or Linux.

4-Axis Eccentric V-Bevel & Zero-Bevel

Dial in perpendicular-torch zero-bevel fit-up on round saddles and rect HSS miters, or cut 4-axis eccentric V-bevel weld preps without a 5-axis tilting torch head.

1,025 Cut Presets + 2D Plate + MCP

Includes 1,025 factory presets across Hypertherm SYNC, Duramax, and Air Plasma, full 2D DXF/SVG flat-sheet nesting in the same window, and local rotarycam-mcp scripting.

03 • Honest Fit

When to Choose GCmaker vs. RotaryCAM

If you already own a new JD Squared table, GCmaker may already be included. For every other table, custom build, or multi-controller shop, RotaryCAM gives you full freedom.

Best Fit For Original JD Squared Table Owners

Choose GCmaker if:

  • You are the original owner of a compatible JD Squared machine where GCmaker is included free with the table.
  • You prefer walk-up parametric tube/joint design and structural NC1 import directly on Windows without a separate 3D CAD package.
  • All of your rotary and structural cutting stays inside the JD Squared ecosystem.
Best Fit For Any 4-Axis Controller, Retrofit & Cross-Platform Shop

Choose RotaryCAM if:

  • You run a custom build, retrofit, or non-JD Squared table on SimCNC, Mach3/4, MASSO, Acorn, QTPlasmaC, FireControl, FlashCut, or WinCNC.
  • You want 100% offline perpetual licensing ($199/$399) on macOS, Windows, or Linux with zero secondary-owner subscriptions.
  • You want 3D STEP/IGES assemblies, 4-axis eccentric V-bevel weld prep, 1,025 cut charts, 2D sheet nesting, and local MCP automation in one app.
04 • FAQ

Common Fabricator Questions

Can I use RotaryCAM on a custom or retrofitted rotary plasma table?

Yes. Pick from 16 built-in controller profiles across 14 dialects, enter your axis velocity/acceleration limits and chuck travel mode, and export tuned G-code immediately.

Does RotaryCAM support structural shapes beyond round and square tube?

Yes. RotaryCAM extracts features directly from 3D STEP/IGES files for round pipe, square/rectangular HSS, angle iron, C-channel, I-beam (with 4-axis beamline emulation), and bent sticks.

Are flat-plate brackets included or do I need a separate nester?

Flat-plate DXF/SVG nesting is built right into RotaryCAM, including recursive Matryoshka part-in-hole packing, common-line sharing, skeleton crop, and Flat-Plate Pro Cuts.

How does Kerf-Crossing THC Auto-Lock prevent torch dives?

RotaryCAM inspects every toolpath spatially and inserts controller-specific THC lock/unlock commands before the torch crosses an existing kerf void or common-line cut.

How does licensing work if I upgrade computers or switch tables?

Your $199 Maker or $399 Pro Shop offline key is perpetual and tied to you, not a specific machine brand or table serial number.
