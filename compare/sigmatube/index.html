02 • Workflow

Where the Two Workflows Differ

No SOLIDWORKS Seat Required

Bring STEP or IGES assemblies from SOLIDWORKS, Fusion, Onshape, Inventor, or FreeCAD, plus 2D DXF/SVG brackets. No expensive CAD add-in lock-in on your shop-floor PC.

Zero-Bevel & 4-Axis V-Bevel Without 5-Axis Hardware

Instead of requiring a 5-axis bevel head, RotaryCAM eliminates overhanging saddle interference via dual-curve zero-bevel pullback and creates eccentric V-groove weld preps on standard 4-axis rotary axes.

Built for Open 4-Axis Plasma Controllers

Skip $12k-$30k+ custom OEM post contracts. Pick from 16 built-in profiles across 14 dialects, including SimCNC 4D trajectory scaling, QTPlasmaC, MASSO G3, Acorn, Mach4, and FireControl.

100x Lower Software Cost

Get 1D interlocking stick nesting, 2D Matryoshka plate nesting, Cut-and-Bend unfolding, and 1,025 plasma presets for $199 or $399 once, offline on macOS, Windows, or Linux.

03 • Honest Fit

When to Choose SigmaTUBE vs. RotaryCAM

SigmaTUBE and RotaryCAM sit in different machine tiers. Choose the tool built for the iron on your shop floor.

Best Fit For Enterprise OEM 5-Axis Tube Lasers

Choose SigmaTUBE if:

  • Your shop runs industrial 5-axis tube lasers or beamlines from BLM, Trumpf, Mazak, or Bystronic needing full machine-envelope simulation.
  • Your engineers work natively inside SOLIDWORKS and require associative model updates and heavy DSTV structural workflows.
  • You already run SigmaNEST ERP/MRP for multi-machine scheduling and automated stock inventory.
Best Fit For 4-Axis Plasma Tables & Job Shops

Choose RotaryCAM if:

  • You run a 4-axis rotary/plate plasma table on SimCNC, MASSO, Acorn, Mach3/4, LinuxCNC QTPlasmaC, or FireControl.
  • You want standalone STEP/IGES + DXF/SVG CAM across all 6 profiles without paying for SOLIDWORKS seats or $12k-$30k+ enterprise quotes.
  • You need zero-bevel copes, 4-axis eccentric V-bevels, Kerf-Crossing THC Auto-Lock, and 1D/2D nesting offline on Mac, Windows, or Linux.
04 • FAQ

Common Fabricator Questions

Can RotaryCAM open SOLIDWORKS weldments without installing a plugin?

Yes. Save your SOLIDWORKS weldment or assembly as .step or .stp and drop it into RotaryCAM. Every tube body, miter, cope, hole, and bend centerline is recognized directly.

How does 4-axis I-beam beamline emulation work?

RotaryCAM classifies web and flange faces on I-beams from 3D STEP files and schedules rotary A-axis indexing and X/Y/Z torch clearances automatically for 4-axis tables.

Can I inspect and adjust pierces, tabs, and nesting interactively?

Yes. You can relocate pierces and micro-tabs on the canvas, drag/rotate/flip 2D parts inside Matryoshka nests, and reorder/flip/roll sticks in 1D pack mode before running the 3D simulator.

Does RotaryCAM support local automation?

Yes. RotaryCAM ships with a local Model Context Protocol server (rotarycam-mcp) so you can automate STEP analysis, machine selection, and G-code exports completely offline.

Are cut charts and post-processors extra add-ons?

No. All 16 controller profiles across 14 dialects and all 1,025 factory plasma presets (Hypertherm SYNC, Duramax, Generic Air Plasma) are included at $199 Maker or $399 Pro Shop.
