Where the Two Products Diverge

03 • Honest Fit

When to Choose Rotary Tube Pro vs. RotaryCAM

Pick the tool that matches your CAD habits, cutting processes, and shop floor controller.

Best Fit For Industrial Multi-Process Tube Tables

Choose Hypertherm Rotary Tube Pro if:

  • You want parametric tube design inside CAM to generate miters, saddles, and hillside branches without opening external 3D CAD.
  • Your floor runs industrial Hypertherm plasma with native SureCut, True Hole, and True Bevel automation, or cuts with laser, waterjet, and oxyfuel.
  • You rely on integrated job costing and purchase software through an OEM or dealer support plan on Windows.
Best Fit For 3D CAD Fab & Mixed Tube/Sheet Tables

Choose RotaryCAM if:

  • You model frames in Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Onshape, or FreeCAD and cut round pipe, HSS, angle iron, channel, I-beam, bent sticks, and DXF/SVG sheet in one app.
  • Your table runs a standard 90-degree torch on SimCNC, LinuxCNC, Mach3/4, MASSO, Acorn, FireControl, or WinCNC and needs tight zero-bevel copes plus 3D simulation.
  • You want offline Mac, Windows, or Linux software with upfront $199/$399 perpetual pricing and zero dealer wait.
04 • FAQ

Common Fabricator Questions

Does RotaryCAM include a built-in parametric pipe designer like Rotary Tube Pro?

No. RotaryCAM imports 3D STEP/IGES parts directly from Fusion 360, SolidWorks, Onshape, and FreeCAD alongside 2D DXF/SVG drawings. If you want to build pipe branches inside CAM without external CAD, Rotary Tube Pro has a parametric designer built in.

Can I cut both structural steel shapes and flat plate brackets in RotaryCAM?

Yes. RotaryCAM handles round pipe, square/rect HSS, angle iron, C-channel, I-beam, cut-and-bend tubes, and 2D flat sheet nesting in the same offline desktop app.

How does zero-bevel compensation help on a perpendicular plasma torch?

A 90-degree torch leaves an angled back wall on steep saddle cuts that stops tubes from seating. RotaryCAM calculates the inner-wall intersection curve and clamps the cut path so coped joints fit cleanly.

Do I need a dealer quote or annual maintenance contract for RotaryCAM?

No. Maker ($199) and Pro Shop ($399) are published one-time perpetual offline licenses with 1 year of updates included. Optional renewals ($59/$99/yr) apply only if you choose future upgrades.

Which CNC plasma controllers work out of the box?

RotaryCAM includes 15 built-in post-processors: SimCNC (with 4D vector feedrate scaling), Mach3, Mach4, LinuxCNC QTPlasmaC, MASSO, Acorn, FireControl, FlashCut, EdingCNC, UCCNC, WinCNC, PlanetCNC, grblHAL, Generic G94, and Air Scribing.
